# ZeroTech 56mm Objective Sunshade for Vengeance 5-25x56 (ZTA-SSVEN5256)

## Summary
A 3-inch (56mm) objective sunshade that threads directly into the objective of the Vengeance 5-25x56 to reduce glare and maintain a clearer sight picture.

## Key features

- **Reduces glare** — limits excess light entering the 56mm objective, especially with the sun low on the horizon.
- **Clearer sight picture** — maintains contrast and viewing comfort in bright conditions.
- **Direct thread fit** — screws straight into the Vengeance 56mm objective; no adapter required.
