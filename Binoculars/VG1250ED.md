## Key features
- **Premium ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass** — eliminates chromatic aberration for razor-sharp resolution and true-to-life colour with strong edge-to-edge sharpness.
- **Dielectrically coated BaK-4 prisms with FBMC lenses** — 88% light transmission for bright, high-contrast images.
- **12x magnification with a large 50mm objective** — maximum reach in the ED line for long-distance and marine observation, with a 4.19mm exit pupil.
- **Waterproof and fog-proof chassis** — dependable on the water and in challenging weather.
- **Rugged ergonomic rubber armour** — shock-resistant and slip-proof, secure in wet, cold, or gloved hands.
- **Fast-focus wheel** — quick, accurate focusing; at 12x, tripod support is recommended for the steadiest views (tripod-supported).
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty.**

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance Binocular manual reference** (`_Vengeance_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
