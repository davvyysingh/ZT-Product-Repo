# ZeroTech Thrive / Thrive HD Binocular — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for ZeroTech Thrive and Thrive HD binoculars (including the Thrive HD Compact). Individual SKU files link here and note model-specific details (magnification, objective, ED/variant).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Unconditional lifetime replacement or repair regardless of fault or reason, for the life of the product. Fully transferable — if you sell the binoculars, the warranty goes with them. (Does not cover intentional loss or damage.)

## Binocular features
Standard Thrive / Thrive HD models: optical tubes, objective lenses, a tube focus adjuster (dioptre, on the right tube), twist-up eye cups, and a central image focus adjuster. The Thrive HD Compact uses an image focus adjuster wheel on top between the tubes and a tube focus adjuster wheel between the eyepieces.

## Adjusting the binoculars to your eyes (standard models)
1. Set the interpupillary width: hold both tubes, raise to your eyes, and move them in/out until you see a single circular field of view — the tubes are now aligned to your eyes.
2. Set the dioptre using an object with detail about 15 m away. The tube focus adjuster is on the right tube, so adjust the left tube first: with your right eye shut, use the central image focus adjuster to make the left-eye image crystal clear, then don't touch it again.
3. With your left eye shut, look through the right tube and use the tube focus adjuster (dioptre) to make the right-eye image crystal clear.
4. Both tubes are now matched to your eyesight; from here, use only the central image focus adjuster to focus at different distances.

## Adjusting the binoculars to your eyes (Compact model)
1. Set the interpupillary width for a comfortable single field of view.
2. With your right eye shut, look through the left eyepiece at an object ~15 m away and use the image focus adjuster wheel (on top, between the tubes) for a sharp image.
3. With your left eye shut, look through the right eyepiece and use only the tube focus adjuster wheel (between the eyepieces) for a sharp image.
4. Thereafter, rotate the image focus adjuster wheel to focus at any distance; twist up the eye cups for comfort as desired.

## Caring for your binoculars
Keep external lenses free of dust and debris (which can scratch the coatings). Clean lenses only with the supplied lens cloth — never tissues or clothing. Protect the binoculars from bumps and drops. Never use chemicals or sprays on any surface (they can damage the rubber armour and lenses). Don't leave the binoculars in direct sunlight for long periods. Never look directly at the sun through binoculars — this will cause serious eye damage.
