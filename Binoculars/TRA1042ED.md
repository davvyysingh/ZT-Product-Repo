## Key features
- **ZeroTech EDX Optical System** — an APO design pairing dual ED glass in the objective with dual lanthanum glass in the eyepiece, with advanced broadband lens coatings and phase-corrected dielectric prisms, for an exceptionally clean and natural image with outstanding centre resolution and sharpness extending towards the edges.
- **HOYA® Japan ED glass** — premium Japanese extra-low dispersion glass reduces unwanted colour dispersion, maintaining clean, natural colour around high-contrast subjects.
- **Up to 94% light transmission** — as the sun drops below the horizon, the remaining ambient light keeps working for you.
- **10x reach with an expansive 7.6° field of view** — 400 ft at 1000 yards; ideal for open country, distant wildlife, birding, coastal observation, mountains and detail at distance.
- **Peak Design carry system included** — a custom ZeroTech x Peak Design Slide Lite strap with Anchor Link system; compact Anchors stay on the binocular so the strap attaches or detaches in seconds, including for tripod use.
- **WeatherShield hydrophobic lens coatings** — bead and shed moisture in rain and mist.
- **Magnesium alloy chassis** — nitrogen filled and waterproof (IPX7), with twist-up eyecups, internal focusing and ~1.5 m close focus.
- **Custom EVA field case** — semi-rigid construction gives a dedicated layer of protection against the knocks and impacts of travel and field use.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty.**
