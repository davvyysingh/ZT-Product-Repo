## Key features
- **ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass** — improved visual sharpness, reduced colour fringing, and a generous depth of field.
- **Dielectrically coated BaK-4 roof prisms with FBMC lenses** — 88% light transmission for bright, high-contrast images.
- **8x magnification with a 42mm objective** — a wide 8.1° field of view and a generous 5.2mm exit pupil for excellent low-light performance.
- **Smooth, fast focus wheel** — quick, precise focusing from 2.5 m to infinity.
- **Full magnesium-alloy construction** — strong yet manageable, with textured grip panels for secure handling.
- **Waterproof, rubber-armoured body** — dependable outdoors; integrated rubber lens covers protect against dust and debris.
- **Flip-top bino caddy harness system** — hands-free carry and convenient storage of essentials while hunting, birding, or hiking.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty.**

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD Binocular manual reference** (`_Thrive_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
