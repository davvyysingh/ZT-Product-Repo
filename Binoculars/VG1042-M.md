## Key features
- **Marine-grade, 100% waterproof and fog-proof** — O-ring sealed and nitrogen-purged to prevent internal fogging and resist water immersion, salt spray, humidity, and heavy rain.
- **10x magnification with a 42mm objective** — a balance of power and field of view for spotting birds, boats, or distant landscapes, with ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass for sharp, true-to-life colour.
- **Fully multi-coated (FMC) lenses and BaK-4 prisms** — brighter, higher-contrast images, including in low light.
- **Non-slip rubber armour** — a secure grip even when wet, with shock resistance against drops and impacts.
- **Floating neck strap included** — keeps the binoculars at the surface if dropped overboard.
- **Wide field of view** — easy tracking of moving subjects on water or land; tripod-supported.
- **BCF-exclusive variant** of the Vengeance 10x42; **unconditional lifetime warranty.**

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance Binocular manual reference** (`_Vengeance_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars; rinse off salt spray and dry before storage
