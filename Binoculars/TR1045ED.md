## Key features
- **ED (Extra-low Dispersion) objective glass** — reduces chromatic aberration for natural, true-to-life colour and crisp detail.
- **Abbe-Koenig prism system** — a premium prism design for superior brightness and contrast.
- **90%+ light transmission with FBMC coatings** — exceptional low-light performance for dawn, dusk, and overcast conditions.
- **10x magnification with a 45mm objective** — extra reach over the 8x45 for long-distance glassing, with edge-to-edge sharpness and a 4.54mm exit pupil.
- **Waterproof, fog-proof, and nitrogen-purged** — all-weather reliability; textured rubber armour for a secure grip.
- **Deeply grooved focus wheel, multi-position eyecups, right-eye dioptre, and a hidden tripod mount** — easy operation and versatility; close focus to 2.5 m.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty.**

## Video insights
A real-world birding review from BJ (tested in changing Tasmanian weather) highlights:
- **Clarity and colour:** ED glass delivers very natural colours that closely match the naked-eye view, avoiding the hazy or unnatural tones of cheaper binoculars — ideal for wildlife such as spotting a black cockatoo at around 100 m.
- **Handling:** Noticeably more manageable and better balanced than larger models like the 15x56, making them easier to carry — a good portable option, including for smaller users.
- **Build:** Solid and durable with a textured finish and a strong protective hard case for transport.
- **Use in the field:** Usable handheld (even with a phone attached via a MagView adapter), with impressive image quality; tripod mounting is supported for steadier viewing — an honest, real-world endorsement of their clarity, natural colour, and practical usability.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace Binocular manual reference** (`_Trace_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Multi-position twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Tripod:** Hidden tripod mount for use on a tripod adapter
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
