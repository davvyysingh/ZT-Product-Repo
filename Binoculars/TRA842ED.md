## Key features
- **ZeroTech EDX Optical System** — ZeroTech's most advanced binocular optical system to date: an APO design pairing dual ED glass in the objective with dual lanthanum glass in the eyepiece, with advanced broadband lens coatings and phase-corrected dielectric prisms.
- **HOYA® Japan ED glass** — premium Japanese extra-low dispersion glass controls different wavelengths of light, reducing unwanted colour dispersion for clean, natural colour around high-contrast subjects.
- **Up to 94% light transmission** — extends useful observation further into twilight and after sunset, while keeping impressive definition towards the edges of the field.
- **Extraordinary 9.1° field of view** — 478 ft at 1000 yards from an 8x42.
- **Peak Design carry system included** — a custom ZeroTech x Peak Design Slide Lite strap with Anchor Link system; compact Anchors stay on the binocular so the strap attaches or detaches in seconds, including for tripod use.
- **WeatherShield hydrophobic lens coatings** — bead and shed moisture in rain and mist.
- **Lightweight magnesium alloy chassis** — nitrogen filled and waterproof (IPX7), with twist-up eyecups, internal focusing and ~1.5 m close focus.
- **Custom EVA field case** — semi-rigid construction gives a dedicated layer of protection against the knocks and impacts of travel and field use.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty.**
