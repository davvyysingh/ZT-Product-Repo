## Key features
- **High Definition (HD) glass** — reduces chromatic aberration for razor-sharp clarity and accurate, natural colour, helping detect subtle movement or camouflaged game in difficult light.
- **Dielectrically coated BaK-4 prisms with FBMC lenses** — maximise brightness and contrast (85% light transmission), even at dawn and dusk.
- **8x magnification with a 42mm objective** — a wide 7.5° field of view and a generous 5.2mm exit pupil for steady glassing and tracking.
- **Fully waterproof and fog-proof** — nitrogen-purged for all-weather performance.
- **Ergonomic rubber armour** — durable, shock-resistant, and grippy with gloves or wet hands; a lightweight chassis (672 g).
- **Fast-focus system** — smooth, responsive focusing with close focus down to 2.5 m; tripod-supported.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty** — no proof of purchase required.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance Binocular manual reference** (`_Vengeance_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
