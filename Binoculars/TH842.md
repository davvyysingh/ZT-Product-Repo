## Key features
- **Fully multi-coated (FMC) lenses** — clear visuals and consistent light transmission for dependable image quality.
- **BaK-4 roof prisms** — a compact, durable optical system with stable, sharp viewing.
- **8x magnification with a 42mm objective** — a classic all-round configuration with a larger 5.3mm exit pupil for better low-light performance than the 32mm model.
- **Waterproof, rubber-armoured construction** — a secure ergonomic grip and added protection in the field.
- **Balanced, comfortable handling (619 g)** — designed for extended observation; tripod-supported for longer sessions.
- **Twist-up eye cups, centre focus, right-tube dioptre** — comfortable use with or without glasses.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty**; includes a flip-top carry bag, shoulder strap, and neck strap.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD Binocular manual reference** (`_Thrive_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
