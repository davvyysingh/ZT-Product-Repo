## Key features
- **8x magnification with a 42mm objective** — a balanced mix of power and field of view for steady, wide-angle viewing, with a generous 5.18mm exit pupil.
- **Fully multi-coated (FMC) optics** — enhanced brightness and sharpness with 80% light transmission.
- **Waterproof and fog-proof sealed construction** — a clear view in changing weather, year-round.
- **Durable rubber-armoured body** — shock-resistant with a secure, non-slip grip for tough field use.
- **Ergonomic centre-focus system with 21mm eye relief** — fast, precise focusing and comfortable viewing (great for glasses wearers).
- **Lightweight (600 g)** — reduced fatigue during extended use; tripod-supported.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty** — backed for life, no proof or paperwork required.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance Binocular manual reference** (`_Vengeance_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
