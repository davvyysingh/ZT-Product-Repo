## Key features
- **ED (Extra-low Dispersion) objective glass** — reduces chromatic aberration for lifelike colour and crisp, high-resolution detail.
- **Abbe-Koenig prism system** — a premium prism design for superior brightness and contrast.
- **90%+ light transmission with FBMC coatings** — exceptional low-light performance for dawn, dusk, dense timber, and overcast conditions.
- **Widest field of view in the ZeroTech range (7.7°)** with edge-to-edge sharpness — clarity from centre to outer rim with a generous 5.78mm exit pupil.
- **Waterproof, fog-proof, and nitrogen-purged** — all-weather reliability; textured rubber armour for a secure grip in wet or muddy conditions.
- **Deeply grooved focus wheel, multi-position eyecups, right-eye dioptre, and a hidden tripod mount** — easy operation and versatility; close focus to 2.5 m.
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty** — no questions asked.

A review from Chris (Scoped Out) rates the Trace 8x45 ED a well-rounded, high-performing optic:
- **Use case:** Offered in 8x45 and 10x45; the 8x45's wider field of view suits general spotting, hunting, and field/3D archery where visibility in complex backgrounds and tricky light matters.

- **Build:** Approximately 975 g, fully waterproof, fog-proof, and nitrogen-purged for all-weather use, with textured rubber armour for a secure grip in wet or muddy conditions.
- **Handling:** An adjustable dioptre on the right eyecup, multi-position eyecups for use with or without glasses, a deeply grooved focus wheel, and a hidden tripod mount.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace Binocular manual reference** (`_Trace_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Multi-position twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Tripod:** Hidden tripod mount for use on a tripod adapter
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
