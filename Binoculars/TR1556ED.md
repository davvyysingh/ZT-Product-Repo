## Key features
- **15x magnification with a large 56mm objective** — powerful long-distance viewing with strong light-gathering for bright images, including at dawn and dusk.
- **ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass** — reduces chromatic aberration for clear, detailed, high-contrast images.
- **Abbe-Koenig prism system with dielectrically coated BaK-4 prisms** — premium brightness and contrast, with class-topping 92% light transmission.
- **Fully broadband multi-coated (FBMC) lenses** — reduced glare and improved contrast across lighting conditions.
- **Waterproof, ergonomic rubber-armoured body** — durable and secure to handle.
- **Smooth, fast focus wheel** — quick, precise focusing at distance; given the 15x power and 1520 g weight, tripod use is recommended for the steadiest views (tripod-supported).
- **Unconditional lifetime warranty**; includes a carry bag, shoulder strap, and neck strap.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace Binocular manual reference** (`_Trace_Binocular_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Eye setup:** Set the interpupillary width for a single circular field of view; the dioptre is on the right tube — focus the left tube first with the centre image focus adjuster (right eye shut), then the right tube with the dioptre (left eye shut); thereafter use the centre focus for all distances
- **Eye cups:** Twist-up, for use with or without glasses
- **Tripod:** Tripod-supported — recommended at 15x for stable viewing (via a tripod adapter)
- **Care:** Clean lenses only with the supplied cloth; no chemicals/sprays; never look at the sun through binoculars
