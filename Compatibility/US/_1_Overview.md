# Red Dot & Prism Compatibility — Overview

Part of the ZeroTech Red Dot & Prism Firearm Compatibility guide (5 files: 1 Overview · 2 Optics & Footprints · 3 Pistol Fitment · 4 Mounts & Adapters · 5 Coverage Summary).

This guide maps every optic in the ZeroTech red dot and prism range (19 SKUs) to the firearms it mounts on. "Compatibility" means the **mounting interface** — whether an optic physically attaches to a firearm, directly or via a plate/adapter. It is not a performance ranking and does not capture every ergonomic or holster consideration. Mounting compatibility is identical in the AU and US markets.

## How a pistol fit is described

- **Direct fit** — the optic bolts straight to the slide's factory cut for that footprint; no plate needed.
- **Direct (multi-cut system)** — the slide's factory multi-footprint pin/plate system natively includes this footprint; no aftermarket parts.
- **Factory plate** — fits using a plate supplied by (or available from) the pistol maker.
- **Adapter plate** — fits only via a third-party adapter plate; the specific plate is named so a claim can reference it exactly.
- **Not suited** — footprint/size mismatch with no sensible path; use the other footprint's optic instead.

## Footprints at a glance

- **Shield RMSc** — slimline / micro-compact pistol standard.
- **Trijicon RMR** — full-size pistol and the versatile rifle/pistol reflex standard.
- **Aimpoint Micro / T-2** — dominant tube-red-dot rail standard.
- **Mini ACOG** — ACOG-pattern base for compact prisms.

## Which optics use which footprint

- **Shield RMSc (pistol):** Thrive HD Micro Reflex "DM21" (THDM21 / THDM21G / THDMM21) and Trace H.A.L.O (TRAE28 / TRAE28G / TRAE28-FDE).
- **Trijicon RMR (pistol & long-gun):** Thrive HD Reflex "RS28" (THDRS28 in red / green / multi-reticle × high & low, plus green multi-reticle FDE — 7 SKUs).
- **Aimpoint Micro / T-2 (rail):** Thrive HD Digital 1x22 (THRD22), Trace R.A.S 1x25 (TRRD125), Thrive Red Dot 3 MOA (THRD25), Thrive HD Micro Prism 1x20 (THDMP120 / -FDE).
- **Mini ACOG (rail):** Thrive HD Variable Prism 1-4x24 (THDP1424).

## Usage note

On any adapter-plate pistol, state compatibility only with the plate named; never imply a bare slide takes the optic. Pistols that host optics only on an integral Picatinny rail (e.g. Desert Eagle, Grand Power Stribog, B&T TP9, some Chiappa Rhino) are not slide-footprint hosts — use a ZeroTech rail optic there.
