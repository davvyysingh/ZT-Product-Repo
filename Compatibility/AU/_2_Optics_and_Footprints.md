## Rifle, carbine & rail-mounted optics

These mount to any firearm with a Picatinny (MIL-STD-1913) or compatible Weaver rail — AR-15/AR-10 & MSRs, tactical lever actions, PCCs, rail-equipped shotguns/bolt guns, and rail-only pistols (those that host optics on an integral rail rather than a slide cut). The RS28 (RMR) also mounts here via its supplied Picatinny mount, besides direct-mounting RMR pistols.
