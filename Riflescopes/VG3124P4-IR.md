# ZeroTech Vengeance 3-12x40 PHR 4 Illuminated (VG3124P4-IR)

## Key features
- **PHR 4 Illuminated reticle** — bold outer posts guide the eye quickly to centre, fine inner lines provide precise aiming points, and six brightness settings adapt as light conditions change.
- **3–12x magnification range** — balanced for bush stalking, range sessions, or long-range confidence.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 15 metres to infinity.
- **0.25 MOA locking turrets** — repeatable, reliable adjustments.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick magnification changes.
- **1-inch aircraft-grade aluminium tube** — lightweight, balanced, and compatible with standard 1" rings.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — clarity, brightness, and contrast in challenging light.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
