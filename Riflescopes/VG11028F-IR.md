## Key features
- **RMG-L Illuminated reticle (FFP, LPVO Specific)** — a central 2 MRAD segmented circle for rapid, precise shot placement; at low magnification it simulates red-dot performance under illumination with heavy bars at the 12, 3, 6, and 9 o'clock positions, expanding into precise holdovers at higher power. Glass-etched, with subtensions consistent across the magnification range.
- **True 1× to 10× magnification** — fast target acquisition up close and clear glass at 10× for extended distance.
- **34mm aircraft-grade aluminium tube (6061-T6)** — strength, reliability, and generous internal adjustment (38 MRAD elevation, 35 MRAD windage).
- **Capped turrets with zero reset** — low-profile, large-diameter turrets indexed in 0.1 MRAD.
- **Wide field of view** — 120 ft at 100 yards on 1× for fast acquisition and strong situational awareness.
- **Locking dioptre** — fine-tune focus, then lock it in for repeatable precision.
- **Argon purged, shockproof, IPX7 waterproof, fully multi-coated lenses** — consistent clarity edge to edge in all conditions.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech walkthrough (Mac from ZeroTech) and an independent unboxing cover the Vengeance HD 1-10x28 FFP LPVO:
- **Build:** Robust 34mm 6061-T6 tube with a 28mm objective, IPX7 waterproofing, and a high-quality anodised finish; available in black (this SKU) and FDE. Compact overall despite the larger tube.
- **Turrets:** Capped windage and elevation turrets with 0.1 mil adjustments, 10 mils per revolution, and 38 mils total internal adjustment; turrets reset to zero once the rifle is zeroed.
- **Reticle and illumination:** The RMG-L is a first-focal-plane illuminated design controlled via a side knob — appearing like a red dot at 1× and expanding into a clean, functional Christmas-tree layout at higher magnification for extended shooting.
- **Handling:** Smooth magnification ring with a removable throw lever and an adjustable locking dioptre with refined knurling and improved ergonomics.
- **Field of view:** A standout — around 120 ft at 1× for near-true-1× performance, fast target acquisition, and excellent situational awareness.
- **Overall:** A durable, compact, high-performance LPVO with excellent glass clarity and a modern reticle, presenting as a premium, well-built option in the competitive 1–10× market. (When installing the battery, remove the protective film to ensure proper contact.)

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance LPVO manual reference** (`_Vengeance_LPVO_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** 2mm hex key (turret centre bolt)
