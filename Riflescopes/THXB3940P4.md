# ZeroTech Thrive X 3-9x40 – PHR 4 (THXB3940P4)

## Summary
A lightweight 30mm-tube, second-focal-plane 3–9×40 hunting scope with a glass-etched PHR 4 reticle and the Adjustable BDC Turret fitted — five shooter-set, lockable distance markers over a full 0.25 MOA scale, with a standard capped turret in the box to switch back — one scope for close, moving game and longer shots across open country at 18.8 oz.

## Key features
- **Glass-etched PHR 4 reticle** — a floating dot for the aiming point with heavy bars to frame it and an open upper field of view, so nothing sits between you and the country you are looking at; quick target acquisition and deliberate shot placement, with holds calibrated at maximum (9x) magnification on this second-focal-plane design.
- **Adjustable BDC Turret included and fitted** — five shooter-set reference markers you can preset or adjust at any time, each individually settable and lockable, with the full 0.25 MOA scale remaining underneath for conventional dialling. Set your drops at the range, dial to the preset marker in the field, and reconfigure on the fly when loads or conditions change.
- **Additional capped elevation turret dial included** — convert back to a simple capped setup whenever you want.
- **All-round 3x to 9x with a fully multi-coated 40mm objective** — covers close, moving game and longer shots across open country; fixed parallax keeps it simple.
- **60 MOA of elevation and windage travel** in 0.25 MOA clicks; turrets run capped or uncapped, with a tool-free coin-slot reset.
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive X and Thrive HD models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **Lightweight build** — 18.8 oz (534 g) including the BDC turret, 43 g (7.5%) lighter than the outgoing Thrive 3-9x40 PHR 4 like for like, so the rifle's balance is barely affected.
- **Proven Thrive platform** — 30mm 6061-T6 aluminium maintube, IPX7 waterproof; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive X** sections, including the Adjustable BDC turret procedures. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Adjustable BDC elevation turret model — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); if the BDC dial's index marks sit slightly off the turret body's index, align them via the three 1.5mm hex screws under the top screw; repeat the reset for windage
- **Setting the BDC markers:** Twist the top dial from Locked to the marker number (1–5), rotate that marker to the desired position, then twist back to Locked
- **Switching turrets:** Supplied standard turret dial and cap fit in place of the BDC turret; the thread protector ring must be fitted when the BDC turret is in place
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; if you reach it while zeroing, reset the dial to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m — no adjustment
- **Lens covers:** Neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
