## Key features
- **RAR Illuminated reticle (SFP)** — a ring-style reticle built for speed: at 1× under illumination it works like a red dot for fast both-eyes-open acquisition, with reference points for holds at higher magnification; the reticle holds a constant size across the magnification range.
- **True 1× to 8× magnification** — fast both-eyes-open acquisition at 1× with the extra reach of 8× for precision at medium distances.
- **Wide field of view** — 124.8 ft at 100 yards on 1× for strong situational awareness and rapid sight alignment.
- **HD optical system** — fully multi-coated lenses for clarity, colour fidelity, and maximum light transmission.
- **Aircraft-grade 7075-T6 aluminium chassis** — high-strength construction with precision seals; IPX7 waterproof.
- **Low-profile capped turrets** — 0.25 MOA clicks for clean, snag-free windage and elevation adjustments.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — flush-fitting, fold flat against the body to prevent snagging.
- **Locking dioptre** and **fixed parallax (100 m)** — simple, repeatable setup; balanced weight for lightweight platforms.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Independent reviews of the Thrive HD 1-8x24 LPVO platform (offered in RAR, G4, and PHR4 reticles):
- **Reticles and illumination:** All reticle options are illuminated; the push-button system offers five brightness settings — a single press activates it and holding the button powers it off while retaining the last-used setting.
- **Turrets:** Capped windage and elevation turrets in quarter-MOA increments (24 MOA per revolution).
- **Build:** 30mm tube with a 24mm objective, 7075-T6 aluminium construction, IPX7 waterproofing, and flush-fit aluminium flip-up caps that fold flat to prevent snagging.
- **Handling:** A standout wide field of view (about 125 ft at 100 yards on 1×) for fast acquisition; clear HD glass that held up moving between bright and cloudy conditions; light enough to suit fast-handling rifles. Reviewers valued the extra reach of 8× over traditional 1–6× LPVOs and liked pairing the optic with an offset red dot for instant close-range transitions.
- **Verdict:** Solid zero at 100 m and reliable performance across lighting conditions, with no major drawbacks noted — a durable, practical, feature-complete LPVO for hunting and dynamic shooting.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** ZeroTech multi-tool — remove the turret centre bolt, re-index to '0'
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click (24 MOA per revolution)
- **Illumination:** Push-button — 5 brightness settings + Off, CR2032 battery (retains last-used setting)
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m — no adjustment
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
