## Key features
- **Clean ZEROPLEX reticle** — a fast, uncluttered sight picture with a traditional feel; on this second-focal-plane design it stays highly visible at every magnification, with holds calibrated at 12x.
- **Versatile 3x to 12x zoom with parallax from 9.2 m** — covers short-range and scrub hunting, leading moving targets and precise shot placement at distance.
- **Fully multi-coated 44mm objective** — bright, clear image optimised for first and last light, without adding bulk to the rifle.
- **Capped 0.25 MOA turrets with 60 MOA of elevation and windage** — protected from unintentional adjustment, yet can be run capped or uncapped; tool-free coin-slot turret reset.
- **Upgrade-ready for the Adjustable BDC Turret** — sold separately; compatible with ZEROPLEX reticle (MOA turret) models only.
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive X and Thrive HD models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **Lightweight 6061-T6 build, IPX7 waterproof** — a sub-20 oz scope that sits light on the rifle; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty, transferable to whoever you hand it to.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac) introduces the Thrive X 3-12x44 family as a lightweight, working-class hunting scope that does everything it needs to without breaking the bank:
- **Layout:** 44mm fully multi-coated objective, 30mm tube, capped windage and capped elevation, side parallax from 10 yards to infinity, an ultra-smooth magnification ring with a stud for the included removable throw lever, and an adjustable dioptre at the rear.
- **Build:** Aircraft-grade 6061-T6 aluminium and IPX7 waterproof, so the scope can be fully submerged — snow, sleet or rain are no concern. Weight starts at 19.8 oz, a sub-20 oz scope suited to lightweight hunting builds and mountain or sheep hunting.
- **Reticles:** A second-focal-plane design offered in five reticles across the family — ZEROPLEX, Mildot, PHR3, PHR4 and G4; reticle holds are true at maximum magnification, while the turrets dial true at any magnification.
- **Turrets:** 60 MOA of internal elevation and windage (17.5 mil on the Mildot version) in ¼ MOA clicks, 14 MOA per revolution (0.1 mil clicks, 10 mil per revolution on the mil version). Low-profile, tactile and audible — not the mushy, quiet turrets expected at the price. Capped, but can be run open; the presenter runs capped windage with an open-top elevation. Tool-free reset: pop out the small wedge with a coin, brass case or knife, slip the turret back to zero and tighten.
- **BDC compatibility:** The same turret design accepts ZeroTech's Adjustable BDC turret — remove the standard turret, fit the taller BDC turret, and run it with capped windage.
- **Windage limiter:** On Thrive X and Thrive HD models the windage turret stops at half a turn so left can't become right; a small steel pin does this. It can be removed by taking the turret off (keep the pin in a bag — it's small), or kept for a set-and-forget zero.
- **In the box:** Neoprene scope cover, the grub screw and the extra cap for running without the BDC turret, lens cloth, reticle chart and warranty card, and a couple of stickers.
- **Verdict:** A lightweight addition to any hunting build for the mountain hunter or whitetail hunter who wants something good, reliable and bright without spending two grand or adding weight.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive X** sections for the turret reset and windage limiter. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Capped elevation turret model — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); repeat for windage
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click (14 MOA per revolution)
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; remove the dial and reset to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **BDC upgrade:** Compatible with the Adjustable BDC Turret (sold separately); fit the thread protector ring when the BDC turret is in place
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 9.2m (10yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** Neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
