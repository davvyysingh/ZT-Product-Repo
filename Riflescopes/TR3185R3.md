## Key features
- **R3 SFP reticle (glass-etched)** — a floating centre dot with 1 MOA subtensions and 4 MOA hash marks for precise holdovers, plus heavy bars at the 3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock positions to guide the eye for faster acquisition.
- **3–18x magnification with 50mm objective and HD glass (92% light transmission)** — bright, clear images for long range and low light, with Weathershield lens coatings.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — rigidity and strong light transmission.
- **Precision Turret System (PTS) with zero stop** — zero-resettable, positive-clicking turrets (0.25 MOA, ~20 MOA per revolution); the windage turret locks; exposed elevation/windage adjust by pulling up, rotating, and locking down.
- **Half Magnification Index (HMI)** — marks the reticle's calibrated half power on the magnification ring.
- **Side parallax (25 m to infinity)** — quick, easy focus with a forgiving eyebox.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Multiple independent reviews assess the Trace 3-18x50 R3 as a strong-value long-range and hunting optic:
- **Build and controls:** Aluminium flip-up covers, a European-style fast-focus eyepiece, and a smooth magnification ring marked at 3×, a half-magnification index, 9×, and 18×. Exposed, zero-resettable elevation and windage turrets (pull up, rotate, lock down) with very positive, tactile clicks; an easy-to-set zero stop; and a locking windage turret.
- **Mechanics:** 30mm tube with roughly 70–80 MOA of adjustment depending on the unit, 20 MOA per revolution, 0.25 MOA clicks, and side parallax from 25 m to infinity that's quick and not finicky, helped by a forgiving eyebox and good eye relief.
- **Optics:** HD glass with around 92% light transmission delivers excellent clarity and brightness with minimal distortion even at 18×.
- **Reticle:** The glass-etched R3 MOA reticle has a fine floating centre dot — clean, non-illuminated, and well-suited to both precise long-range work and hunting.
- **Real-world use:** Reviewers ran it on a 6.5 Creedmoor out to 1,000 m and on a .270 for hunting, taking deer, foxes, sambar, and fallow — concluding it's a proven, reliable, well-built all-rounder that punches above its mid-range price, backed by the lifetime warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); locking windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click (~20 MOA per revolution)
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 25m (27yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
