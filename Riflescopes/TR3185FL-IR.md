## Key features
- **FFP LR Hunter Illuminated reticle (glass-etched)** — a hybrid design combining the familiarity of traditional reticles at low magnification with the precision needed for long range at higher powers; a fine centre dot and precise MRAD subtensions for accurate elevation and windage corrections, with adjustable illumination for changing light.
- **3–18x magnification with 50mm objective and HD glass (92% light transmission)** — bright, clear images for long range and low light, with Weathershield lens coatings.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — rigidity and strong light transmission.
- **Precision Turret System (PTS) with zero stop** — precise, positive adjustments (0.1 MRAD) and a reliable return to zero, with a capped windage turret to guard against accidental changes.
- **Side parallax (25 m to infinity)** — sharp focus across distances.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Illuminated** model, built on the globally popular 4.5-27x50 FFP platform; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); capped windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Illuminated reticle — adjustable brightness, CR2032 battery
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 25m (27yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
