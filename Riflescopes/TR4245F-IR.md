## Key features
- **FFP RMG Illuminated reticle (glass-etched)** — a first-focal-plane MRAD reticle developed with HORUS Vision, with clear holdover references for both speed and precision and reticle illumination for low-light use; the TREMOR3 reticle is also available on this model.
- **4–24x magnification with 50mm objective** — a versatile precision and PRS range, with a 30mm one-piece main tube for structural strength and consistent optics.
- **Precision Turret System (PTS) with industry-leading zero stop** — reliable, repeatable, tactile adjustments (0.1 MRAD) and confident return to zero, with a capped precision windage turret for a clean, protected profile.
- **Side parallax (20 m to infinity)** — firm and precise, down to close range.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — fast power changes.
- **Tenebraex Tactical Tough flip-up covers** and a **50mm sunshade** — lens protection and glare control in harsh environments.
- **Argon purged, Weathershield lens coating, IPX7 waterproof** — durability and clarity in all conditions.
- **Made in Japan**; illuminated model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Independent reviews — including a long-term review — assess the Japanese-made Trace Advanced 4-24x50 as a strong PRS-style optic:
- **Build:** Excellent build quality with a durable matte finish, a well-balanced magnification ring with a removable throw lever, and a 30mm tube for broad mounting compatibility; side parallax down to 20 m, firm enough to avoid accidental movement. Relatively lightweight for a long-range optic at around 790 g.
- **Optics:** A standout — high-resolution glass with accurate colour, strong contrast, and no noticeable chromatic aberration, supported by argon purging and weather-resistant coatings.
- **Reticle options:** Offered with glass-etched, first-focal-plane TREMOR3 and (illuminated) RMG reticles for precise holdovers and fast adjustments.
- **Turrets:** Tactile, easy to read, with a zero-stop system and reliable tracking confirmed through repeated testing; locking turrets with dependable return to zero. (A rotation indicator was noted as a possible addition.)
- **Accuracy:** On a 20" AR (PSA), it delivered tight groups — most around an inch or tighter with a couple near 3/8" — and made consistent hits at 630–830 yards including in low light, with reviewers rating it capable to 1,000 yards and excellent value short of ultra-premium pricing.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); capped windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Mechanical dial — 10 brightness settings, CR2032 battery (an "Off" position between each setting)
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 20m (22yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers / sunshade:** Tenebraex Tactical Tough flip-up covers; 50mm sunshade included
