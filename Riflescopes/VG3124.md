A lightweight 1-inch, second-focal-plane 3–12×40 with the simple ZEROPLEX reticle, Pop-Lok 0.25 MOA turrets, and side parallax — a fast-handling scrub and bush hunting scope.

## Key features
- **ZEROPLEX SFP reticle** — a clean, simple reticle for high precision and fast target acquisition without needing to dial the turret.
- **3–12x magnification range** — versatile reach for scrub, bush, and rimfire shooting.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 15 metres to infinity.
- **Pop-Lok 0.25 MOA turrets** — repeatable, lockable adjustments.
- **1-inch 6061-T6 aluminium tube** — lightweight and balanced; fits standard rings.
- **Fast-focus dioptre** — quick reticle sharpening.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — dependable field performance.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
