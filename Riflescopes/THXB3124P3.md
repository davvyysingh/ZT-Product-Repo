# ZeroTech Thrive X 3-12x44 – PHR 3 (THXB3124P3)

## Summary
A lightweight 30mm-tube, second-focal-plane 3–12×44 hunting scope with a glass-etched PHR 3 reticle and the Adjustable BDC Turret fitted — five shooter-set, lockable distance markers over a full 0.25 MOA scale, with a standard capped turret in the box to switch back — so one scope covers close, moving game and longer shots across open country.

## Key features
- **Glass-etched PHR 3 reticle** — quick target acquisition and a fast, uncluttered aim in low light, promoting efficient, deliberate shot placement; on this second-focal-plane design the reticle holds are calibrated at maximum (12x) magnification.
- **Adjustable BDC Turret included and fitted** — five shooter-set reference markers you can preset or adjust at any time, each individually settable and lockable, with the full 0.25 MOA scale remaining underneath for conventional dialling. Set your drops at the range, dial to the preset marker in the field, and reconfigure on the fly when loads or conditions change.
- **Standard capped turret in the box** — convert back to a simple capped setup whenever you want.
- **Versatile 3x to 12x zoom with parallax from 9.2 m** — covers close, moving game and longer shots across open country; the fully multi-coated 44mm objective keeps the image bright without adding bulk.
- **60 MOA of elevation and windage travel** in 0.25 MOA clicks; turrets run capped or uncapped, with a tool-free coin-slot reset.
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive X and Thrive HD models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **Proven Thrive platform** — 30mm 6061-T6 aluminium maintube, IPX7 waterproof; only 20.2 oz (574 g) including the BDC turret, so the rifle's balance is barely affected; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac) introduces the Thrive X 3-12x44 family as a lightweight, working-class hunting scope that does everything it needs to without breaking the bank:
- **Layout:** 44mm fully multi-coated objective, 30mm tube, capped windage and capped elevation, side parallax from 10 yards to infinity, an ultra-smooth magnification ring with a stud for the included removable throw lever, and an adjustable dioptre at the rear.
- **Build:** Aircraft-grade 6061-T6 aluminium and IPX7 waterproof, so the scope can be fully submerged — snow, sleet or rain are no concern. Weight starts at 19.8 oz for the family, suited to lightweight hunting builds and mountain or sheep hunting.
- **Reticles:** A second-focal-plane design offered in five reticles across the family — ZEROPLEX, Mildot, PHR3, PHR4 and G4; reticle holds are true at maximum magnification, while the turrets dial true at any magnification.
- **Turrets:** 60 MOA of internal elevation and windage (17.5 mil on the Mildot version) in ¼ MOA clicks, 14 MOA per revolution on the standard turret. Low-profile, tactile and audible — not the mushy, quiet turrets expected at the price. Capped, but can be run open; the presenter runs capped windage with an open-top elevation. Tool-free reset: pop out the small wedge with a coin, brass case or knife, slip the turret back to zero and tighten.
- **BDC turret:** The same turret design accepts ZeroTech's Adjustable BDC turret — the taller turret with the BDC markings fits in place of the standard one, and the presenter runs it with capped windage and the BDC turret on top; the standard turret can be refitted and capped or run open.
- **Windage limiter:** On Thrive X and Thrive HD models the windage turret stops at half a turn so left can't become right; a small steel pin does this. It can be removed by taking the turret off (keep the pin in a bag — it's small), or kept for a set-and-forget zero.
- **In the box:** Neoprene scope cover, the grub screw and the extra cap for running without the BDC turret, lens cloth, reticle chart and warranty card, and a couple of stickers.
- **Verdict:** A lightweight addition to any hunting build for the mountain hunter or whitetail hunter who wants something good, reliable and bright without spending two grand or adding weight.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive X** sections, including the Adjustable BDC turret procedures. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Adjustable BDC elevation turret model — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); if the BDC dial's index marks sit slightly off the turret body's index, align them via the three 1.5mm hex screws under the top screw; repeat the reset for windage
- **Setting the BDC markers:** Twist the top dial from Locked to the marker number (1–5), rotate that marker to the desired position, then twist back to Locked
- **Switching turrets:** Supplied standard turret dial and cap fit in place of the BDC turret; the thread protector ring must be fitted when the BDC turret is in place
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; if you reach it while zeroing, reset the dial to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 9.2m (10yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** Neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
