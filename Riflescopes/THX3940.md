## Key features
- **Clean ZEROPLEX reticle** — a simple, uncluttered sight picture that lends itself to fast-paced, "point and shoot" hunting; on this second-focal-plane design the reticle stays the same size at every power, with holds calibrated at 9x.
- **All-round 3x to 9x magnification** — handles close, moving game and open-country distance shooting alike; the 40mm fully multi-coated objective gives a clear, even image across the range.
- **Lightweight construction** — at 18.3 oz it sits light on the rifle, so you feel it less over a long day on foot.
- **Capped 0.25 MOA turrets with 60 MOA of elevation and windage** — establish your zero and go hunting, with nothing to knock or re-dial in the field; run capped or uncapped, with a tool-free coin-slot reset.
- **Fixed parallax** — keeps it simple by design, with 84mm of eye relief to keep you clear of recoil.
- **Upgrade-ready for the Adjustable BDC Turret** — sold separately; fits this ZEROPLEX (MOA turret) model for user-configurable bullet-drop settings matched to your own load and distances (check the packaging QR fit chart).
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive X and Thrive HD models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **30mm 6061-T6 aluminium maintube, IPX7 waterproof** — shrugs off rain and creek crossings; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive X** sections for the turret reset and windage limiter. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Capped elevation turret model — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); repeat for windage
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; if you reach it while zeroing, reset the dial to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **BDC upgrade:** Compatible with the Adjustable BDC Turret (sold separately); fit the thread protector ring when the BDC turret is in place
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m — no adjustment
- **Lens covers:** Neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
