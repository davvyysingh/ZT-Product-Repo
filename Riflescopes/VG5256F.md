## Key features
- **FFP RMG reticle (glass-etched, MRAD)** — designed in-house for PRS and long-range shooters; holds elevation and windage for fast, precise shots or dials for distance, with a clean Christmas-tree layout, fine centre aiming dot, and clear mil markings that stay uncluttered.
- **5–25x magnification with 56mm objective** — high magnification and strong light gathering for confident long-range work and low light.
- **34mm one-piece maintube** — increased internal adjustment (28 MRAD elevation, 20 MRAD windage) and rigidity.
- **ZeroStop turrets (0.1 MRAD)** — tactile, precise clicks with a reliable mechanical zero stop and confident return to zero.
- **Side parallax down to 15 metres** — suits long range as well as close-range disciplines like .22 PRS and air rifle.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick power changes.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses with Weathershield protection, IPX7 waterproof** — clarity, contrast, and durability.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Multiple independent reviews assess the Vengeance 5-25x56 FFP as a PRS and long-range optic that performs above its price:
- **Accuracy and tracking:** On a Savage Model 10 in .308 (175gr SMK) it produced tight groups and accurate hits beyond 1,000 yards with outstanding tracking and repeatability. A dedicated return-to-zero test — running the turrets to their stops and back repeatedly between shots — kept impacts on the same point of aim.
- **Turrets (standout):** Tactile, precise clicks with excellent grip and a reliable mechanical zero stop; firm enough to resist accidental adjustment and easy to set.
- **Optics:** The 56mm objective gives strong low-light brightness near sunset; glass is rated above average, staying clear at full magnification with minimal chromatic aberration and strong edge clarity.
- **Reticle:** The RMG offers a fine centre aiming dot, clear mil-based holdovers, and a well-structured Christmas-tree layout (around 10 mils of hold vertically and horizontally) that aids corrections without cluttering the view.
- **Build and value:** A 34mm tube for added adjustment range and solid, "tank-like" construction (on the heavier side, around 2.4 lb). Reviewers compared it favourably to higher-end optics such as the Trace Advanced and Nightforce NX8, calling it an excellent, well-priced entry into precision shooting backed by ZeroTech's lifetime warranty. (One reviewer's early sample didn't include the throw lever in the box, though it is listed as included.)
