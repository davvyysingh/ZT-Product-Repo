## Key features
- **PHR 4 Illuminated SFP reticle** — a clean floating centre dot with subtle reference points for rapid acquisition and precise holds, with push-button digital illumination across six brightness levels (returns to the last-used setting).
- **True 1× to 10× magnification** — fast both-eyes-open target acquisition for close quarters, with a full 10× zoom for confidence at distance.
- **24mm fully multi-coated objective** — enhanced light transmission and reduced glare.
- **30mm 6061-T6 aircraft-grade tube** — lightweight (17.8 oz) yet robust, IPX7 waterproof, fog-proof, and shockproof.
- **Capped windage and elevation turrets** — 0.25 MOA clicks with 120 MOA of internal adjustment for a clean, snag-free build.
- **Integrated removable throw lever** — smooth, fast magnification changes.
- **Locking dioptre** and **fixed parallax (125 m)** — simple, repeatable setup.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac from ZeroTech) presents the Thrive 1-10x24 LPVO as a no-frills, reliable optic for gas guns or lightweight hunting rifles:
- **Optics and build:** 24mm fully multi-coated objective for light transmission and glare reduction, on a durable 30mm 6061-T6 tube that's IPX7 waterproof, fog-proof, and shockproof.
- **Turrets:** Capped elevation and windage turrets offering 120 MOA of adjustment.
- **Illumination:** Push-button illumination with six brightness settings that cycle easily and return to the last-used setting.
- **Handling:** True 1× performance with a wide field of view (around 118 ft at 100 yards) for fast acquisition, up to 10× when needed; a smooth, secure, removable throw lever and an adjustable dioptre. Fixed parallax at 125 metres.
- **Reticles and package:** A second-focal-plane optic available in RA, G4, and PHR4 reticle options; ships with a neoprene cover, warranty information, stickers, lens cloth, and a see-through bungee cover — a practical, lightweight, easy-to-use package for AR platforms or lightweight hunting setups.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** ZeroTech multi-tool — remove the turret centre bolt, re-index to '0'
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Push-button digital — six brightness settings, CR2032 battery (returns to last-used setting)
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 125m — no adjustment
