## Key features
- **First Focal Plane RMG reticle** — reticle scales with magnification for constant elevation and windage holds at any power; subtended 0.5 mil increments and 0.1 mil measuring bars, with a floating centre dot and heavy chevron bars for visibility and accuracy across magnifications.
- **ZeroStop turrets** — reliable return to zero after dialling, for a quick field reference.
- **6–24x magnification with 50mm objective** — high magnification and strong light gathering for long range.
- **30mm one-piece maintube** — mounting rigidity and consistent light transmission.
- **Internal adjustment** — 17.5 MRAD elevation and 17.5 MRAD windage.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick magnification changes.
- **6061-T6 maintube, FMC lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — durable and weather-sealed.
- **0.1 MRAD turret index.**
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent video review pairs the Vengeance 6-24x50 FFP RMG with a Savage 110 Elite Precision in 6.5 Creedmoor:
- **Long-range accuracy:** Produced a half-MOA five-shot group at 1,000 yards with factory ammunition — nearly a personal record — with the reviewer describing the rifle/optic combination as one that "absolutely hammers."
- **Reticle and versatility:** The RMG reticle is rated excellent; the scope is seen as sitting ideally in the lineup, doing many things well across PRS, long-range, and hunting use. At 14x the image stays clear and usable.
- **Tracking and parallax:** Impacts were easy to hear and track at 300 yards; performance held at 627 yards and beyond, with the side parallax working well and delivering excellent groups.
- **Glass quality:** Clear image with impressive contrast — even difficult colours and targets stayed sharp and easy to distinguish.
- **Low light:** Continued to perform well in low light, with consistent, easy-to-spot impacts at 630 yards and excellent clarity at the 1,000-yard line for spotting trace and impacts.
