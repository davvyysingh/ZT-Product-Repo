# ZeroTech Vengeance — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for all ZeroTech Vengeance riflescopes. Individual SKU files link here and note which model-specific sections apply (SFP vs FFP turret procedure, MOA vs MRAD click value, illumination, sunshade eligibility).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar product and returns it to you at no charge.

## Setting the dioptre (do this first)
Set magnification to maximum and, if fitted, the parallax turret to the infinity marker. Look briefly (no more than 5 seconds at a time) at a blank, featureless and bright surface such as a white wall or clear blue sky — never look directly at the sun. If the reticle isn't immediately sharp and clear, twist the dioptre adjuster until it is; the dioptre adjuster has approximately 1¾ turns of adjustment. ZeroTech recommends starting with the dioptre flush against the ocular and adjusting anticlockwise (toward the + symbol) until optimal. Note the setting for future reference.

## Mounting
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present. ZeroTech recommends professional mounting to preserve the warranty. Use quality rings/bases; ensure the scope slides freely (lap rings if needed); avoid rings contacting the maintube/erector, magnification-ring or objective junctions; confirm no part of the scope touches the barrel or receiver. Tighten ring tops to 18 in lbs. A bubble level is highly recommended for consistent reticle alignment.

## Adjusting parallax (side focus)
With the rifle well supported, use the parallax turret to obtain the sharpest target image and a refined point of aim. Move your head gently up/down — if the reticle drifts from the aim point, adjust the turret until it stays fixed. Repeat for different distances; the distance markings are a guide only.

## Zeroing
ZeroTech recommends zeroing most centrefire rifles at approximately 100 metres +/- 10 metres; rimfire hunting rifles are most commonly zeroed at 50 – 75 metres depending on the ammunition. Bore sight first. Dialling the elevation turret toward the **'U'** symbol moves impact up; the windage turret toward the **'R'** symbol moves it right.

Click values per the manual:
- **MOA turrets:** ¼ Minutes of Angle (0.25 MOA) adjust bullet impact approximately 7-8mm at 100 metres (approx. ¼" at 100 yards) per click.
- **MRAD turrets:** 0.1 Milrad (0.1 MRAD) adjust bullet impact 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click.

## Resetting the turrets after zeroing — SFP models
(3-12x40, 4-16x40, 4.5-18x40, 4-20x50, 6-24x50.) With the elevation turret locked, loosen the 3mm Allen-key keeper on top and set aside. Slide the turret off and reposition so the '0' index lines up with the vertical index mark on the maintube. Refit and tighten the keeper, confirming the '0' position. Repeat for the windage turret.

## Setting the zero stop and resetting the turrets — FFP models
(4-16x44, 6-24x50, 5-25x56.) Once zeroed, remove the windage and elevation turrets using a 2mm hex key — there are three 2mm grub screws in each turret. Loosen each grub screw 2 revolutions and pull up on the turret to expose the zero stop assembly on the elevation turret (the windage turret has no zero stop). Loosen the three grub screws in the orange zero stop ring so it rotates freely; align the zero stop pin on the bottom of the ring with the right side of the stop lug on the lower assembly so the pin contacts the lug; retighten the three grub screws. Refit the turret cap with the vertical index mark on the revolution scale aligned to the '0', and tighten the three 2mm grub screws (approximately 6 in lbs). Use the same process for the windage turret, disregarding the zero stop.

## Attaching the sunshade — 4-20x50 & 5-25x56 only
These models accept the sunshade (part ZTA-SSVEN5256) — fit it to the objective and tighten firmly.

## Reticle illumination — illuminated models
Illuminated reticles have six brightness settings with an 'Off' position between each setting. Before first use, remove the illumination dial battery cap and discard the plastic circuit blocker, then reinstall the CR 2032 button battery and refit the cap. Switch illumination to 'Off' when the rifle is in storage or transport, and carry a spare CR 2032 button battery when relying on the illumination feature.

## Battery safety warning — illuminated models
Illuminated models use a CR 2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Scope care
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth.
