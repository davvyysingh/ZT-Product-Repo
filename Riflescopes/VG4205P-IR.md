## Key features
- **PHR 2 Illuminated reticle** — refined floating centre dot with a fine 6 o'clock post for clean aiming points and precise elevation holds; illumination improves target contrast in low light without cluttering the sight picture.
- **4–20x magnification with 50mm objective** — excellent clarity and brightness at distance.
- **30mm one-piece tube (6061-T6)** — strength, stability, and consistent optical alignment.
- **Pop-Lok 0.25 MOA turrets** — tactile, repeatable feedback.
- **Fast-focus dioptre** — quick reticle adjustment for different users.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — reliable real-world performance.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent video review covers the Vengeance 4-20x50 (illuminated PHR2 configuration) as a budget-friendly yet feature-rich optic for hunting and general shooting:
- **Build and packaging:** Built on a 30mm tube with a premium-looking finish and durable coating; well-packaged with a scope cover, manual, lens cloth, and tools. The PHR2 illuminated reticle is hunting-focused with useful holdover references.
- **Controls:** Side parallax adjustment, locking windage and elevation turrets, a smooth easy-to-use dioptre, and a raised magnification knob for improved handling. Turrets are resettable via a simple single Allen-screw system.
- **Versatility:** The 4x–20x range suits both close and extended distances, from brush country to longer-range shooting.
- **Illumination:** Six brightness levels with off positions between each setting; the reviewer felt it suited daylight use and could appear slightly bright in low light.
- **Optical quality:** Clear glass and reliable performance for the price; proved capable in low-light hunting with accurate shot placement.
