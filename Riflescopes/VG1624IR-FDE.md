## Key features
- **RAR SFP Illuminated reticle** — illuminated horseshoe/circle and centre dot for fast close-range acquisition, with 2 MOA hash marks for holdovers and basic ranging; uncluttered design without a full Christmas-tree layout.
- **True 1× to 6× magnification** — both-eyes-open speed up close with reach to 6× for mid-range shots.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strong mounting versatility and durability, in a Flat Dark Earth finish.
- **Low-profile capped 0.5 MOA turrets** — precise, audible indexing with consistent return to zero; 80 MOA total internal adjustment.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick power changes.
- **Locking dioptre** — secure the setting for a repeatable 1× image.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — clarity, contrast, and reliable sealing.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Two independent video reviews assess the Vengeance 1-6x24 LPVO:
- **Durability:** Mounted on an AR-10 in .308, it handled both rearward recoil and forward bolt impact without issue, demonstrating solid internal construction.
- **Range and reticle:** The 1–6× range proved effective from roughly 7–10 yards out to 500 metres. The SFP RAR reticle's illuminated horseshoe and centre dot drive fast acquisition, while 2 MOA hash marks support holdovers and basic ranging (holdovers are accurate at maximum magnification, as expected of SFP).
- **Turrets and tracking:** Large capped 0.5 MOA turrets give positive, tactile clicks that are easy to feel with gloves; tracking was reliable within practical distances, and turrets are resettable to zero with shooter-facing directional markings — primarily a set-and-forget design.
- **Optics and ergonomics:** Bright, sharp image with good low-light transmission (slight edge falloff noted); roughly 3.9" eye relief at 1× with a solid eyebox; smooth, well-balanced magnification ring with removable throw lever (the magnification indicator sits in a slightly awkward spot).
- **Form factor:** 30mm tube, around 9.5 inches long and just over 20 ounces. Across hundreds of rounds on multiple platforms it proved reliable in transitions, barricade work, and general plinking — a capable, affordable LPVO standing out on features and usability.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance LPVO manual reference** (`_Vengeance_LPVO_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** 2mm hex key (turret centre bolt)
- **Turret index:** 0.5 MOA — approx. 15mm at 100 metres (0.5" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** RAR Illuminated — six brightness settings, CR2032 battery
- **Parallax:** Fixed — no adjustment
