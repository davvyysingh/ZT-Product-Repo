## Key features
- **R3 reticle** — clear, intuitive aiming points for accurate holdovers at varying distances; a straightforward layout for fast target acquisition without cluttering the sight picture.
- **4–20x magnification with 50mm objective** — bright, detailed images across a wide range of distances.
- **30mm one-piece tube (6061-T6)** — strength, stability, and consistent optical alignment.
- **Pop-Lok 0.25 MOA turrets** — tactile, repeatable performance.
- **Fast-focus dioptre** — quick reticle sharpening for individual users.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — clarity, contrast, and reliable real-world performance.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent video review tests the Vengeance 4-20x50 R3 (an MOA-based second-focal-plane optic) on an air rifle for tracking and close range, then a 6.5 Creedmoor for long range:
- **Zeroing and tracking:** Zeroed quickly within just a few shots with excellent turret responsiveness.
- **Turrets (standout):** Solid, tactile feel with good grip from detailed knurling and scalloped edges; a lift-to-adjust and push-to-lock system prevents accidental movement and aligns cleanly at zero. The reviewer notes this model lacks a zero stop and illumination, and the absence of revolution-indicator markings means users should stay mindful of their adjustments.
- **Glass and handling:** Smooth magnification ring with a raised throw lever; the 50mm objective gives strong light transmission and clear glass even in haze and low light.
- **Long range:** Maintained excellent clarity beyond 1000 yards, letting the shooter spot impacts and trace shots in grass and low-contrast environments. The side parallax (close to infinity) proved highly effective for sharpening the image.
- **Overall:** Performed well near sunset and felt more premium than its price suggests, with smooth controls and solid build quality — recommended for both target shooting and hunting at extended ranges.
