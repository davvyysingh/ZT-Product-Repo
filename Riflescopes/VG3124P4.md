## Key features
- **PHR 4 reticle** — bold outer posts for fast target acquisition combined with fine inner lines for precise aiming and accurate leads.
- **3–12x magnification range** — an ideal balance for bush stalking, range sessions, or long-range precision.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — corrects parallax error from 15 metres to infinity.
- **Lockable, zero-reset 0.25 MOA turrets** — precise, repeatable corrections that hold zero after extended field use.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick magnification changes.
- **1-inch 6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium tube** — lightweight, balanced, and compatible with standard 1" rings.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — crisp contrast and bright images in all light conditions.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
