## Key features
- **R3 SFP reticle (glass-etched)** — a floating centre dot with MOA subtensions and hash marks for precise holdovers, plus heavy bars at the 3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock positions to guide the eye for faster acquisition. Also available in RMG and RMG MOA.
- **4.5–27x magnification with 50mm objective and ~92% light transmission** — a wide range for long range, range, and backcountry hunting with bright, clear images.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strength and superior light transmission, with Weathershield lens coatings.
- **Locking elevation and windage turrets with zero stop** — lift to adjust, press to lock; up to 60 MOA of adjustment in 0.25 MOA clicks with a reliable return to zero.
- **Side parallax (25 m to infinity)** — smooth focus across distances.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A review (Scoped Out) covers the Trace 4.5-27x50 R3 specifically, alongside broader platform reviews:
- **Configuration:** A second-focal-plane optic on a 30mm tube with a 50mm objective and side parallax from 25 m to infinity.
- **Turrets:** Locking elevation and windage turrets — lift to adjust, press to secure — with up to 60 MOA of adjustment in 0.25 MOA increments and a built-in zero stop for a quick return to zero without over-rotating.
- **Reticle:** The R3 second-focal-plane reticle is glass-etched for durability and precision, with a fine centre aiming point and a clean, uncluttered layout; thicker outer stadia aid acquisition at lower magnification.
- **Optics and build:** Weathershield coatings and around 92% light transmission give bright, clear images in lower light; rugged construction with included HD alloy flip-up covers, tools, and spare grub screws, backed by the lifetime warranty.
- **Platform note:** Broader reviews of the 4.5-27x50 (in its RMG/MRAD form) confirmed consistent tracking with no point-of-impact shift, firm tactile clicks, a precisely machined zero stop, and good value — manufactured in China to rugged Australian standards.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); locking windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 25m (27yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
