## Key features
- **Illuminated glass-etched PHR 2 reticle calibrated for 24x** — unobtrusive holdover stadia out to 20 MOA on this second-focal-plane design; clean, crisp lines for efficient, deliberate shot placement, with red illumination across six brightness settings to manage low light.
- **Adjustable BDC Turret included and fitted** — five independently settable, lockable distance markers built from your own confirmed drops, with the full 0.25 MOA scale remaining underneath for conventional dialling. Set your drops at the range, dial to the preset marker in the field, and reconfigure on the fly when loads or conditions change.
- **Standard ¼ MOA turret dial and cap included** — for those who prefer a more traditional configuration; turrets run capped or uncapped.
- **6x to 24x magnification** — 24x resolves the detail that decides a long shot, with an 8.3mm exit pupil at 6x for comfort behind the scope.
- **Parallax adjustable from 9.2 m to infinity**, 95mm of eye relief, and a 50mm fully multi-coated objective for first and last light.
- **60 MOA of elevation and windage travel** in 0.25 MOA clicks from a 30mm 6061-T6 maintube; tool-free coin-slot turret reset.
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive HD and Thrive X models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **Top-tier performance tailored for precision hunting** — 23.0 oz (653 g) for a 6-24, 4.0 oz lighter than the previous generation with covers and adapters fitted; IPX7 sealed and fogproof; heavy-duty aluminium flip-up lens covers fitted; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty, transferable with no paperwork.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk presents the updated Thrive HD 6-24x50 as a light scope with high magnification for backcountry hunters — a 6-24 at about 22 oz:
- **Layout:** Fully multi-coated 50mm objective; 30mm tube; capped windage with a choice of elevation setups; 60 MOA of elevation and windage in ¼ MOA clicks, 15 MOA per revolution; IPX7 so it can be fully submerged; a buttery-smooth magnification ring with a position for the included removable throw lever; adjustable dioptre; 6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium body.
- **Parallax and illumination:** Side parallax from 10 yards to infinity; on the illuminated model a button beside it — press to turn on, tap to cycle six brightness settings, hold 3–5 seconds to turn off, and a single tap brings it back at the previous setting.
- **Reticles:** Second focal plane, so the reticle stays the same size at any power and its holds are true at maximum magnification while the turrets work at any power; offered in PHR 2 and PHR 2 Illuminated.
- **In the box:** Neoprene scope cover, standard turret and cap, grub screws and Allen key, stickers, lens cloth and reticle chart.
- **Windage limiter:** Not a zero stop — a small pin that stops the windage dial going a full revolution so "L" and "R" aren't confused (14 MOA of travel, switching at 7 each side). If you run into it while zeroing, lift the dial, set it back to zero, continue adjusting, then lock the cap down; the pin can be removed with a small flat-head screwdriver if you'd rather run without it.
- **BDC turret:** Ships with two elevation options — the capped-style standard turret, still dialable under its cap, or the ZeroTech Adjustable BDC turret. Unlike a laser-engraved distance dial it keeps the ¼ MOA scale, so environmental changes (the presenter zeroes at 4,000 ft and hunts at 8–10,000 ft) can still be dialled, while five adjustable markers can be set to known distances for a match stage or to several glassed clearings; the top dial unlocks one marker at a time and a lock setting holds them all. Tool-free and adjustable in the field.
- **Verdict:** The Thrive HD line has long been among the presenter's favourite hunting scopes for weight, clarity and durability; the new BDC turret fixes the one thing he liked least about them, the turret system.
## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive HD Adjustable BDC Turret (THDB)** sections for the turret reset, BDC procedures and windage limiter. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Adjustable BDC elevation turret — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); if the BDC dial's index marks sit slightly off the turret body's index, align them via the three 1.5mm hex screws under the top screw; repeat the reset for windage
- **Setting the BDC markers:** Twist the top dial from Locked to the marker number (1–5), rotate that marker to the desired position, then twist back to Locked
- **Switching turrets:** Supplied standard turret dial and cap fit in place of the BDC turret; the thread protector ring must be fitted when the BDC turret is in place
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; if you reach it while zeroing, reset the dial to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **Illumination:** Push-button on the parallax turret — 5 brightness settings + Off, CR2032 battery under the screw-on cover (3-hour auto-off, memory function)
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 9.2m (10yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** Heavy-duty aluminium flip-up covers (fitted) plus a neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
