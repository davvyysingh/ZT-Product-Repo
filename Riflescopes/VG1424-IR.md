## Key features
- **RAR MOA Illuminated reticle** — heavy outer bars guide the eye to centre, with a segmented illuminated circle and centre dot, 2 MOA hash marks, and 4 MOA holdover intervals for fast transitions and precise holds; six brightness levels for any light.
- **True 1× magnification** — both-eyes-open shooting and fast target transitions, extending to 4× for added reach.
- **1–4x magnification with 24mm objective** — built for close to mid-range engagement.
- **30mm 6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium tube** — robust, lightweight, and field-ready.
- **0.5 MOA capped turrets** — protected adjustments with 120 MOA total internal travel.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — clear, high-contrast images and reliable sealing.
- **Locking dioptre** — lock in the optimal setting for repeatable clarity.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech product walkthrough (presented by Mac from ZeroTech) covers the Vengeance 1-4x LPVO:
- **Build:** Compact and practical on a 30mm 6061-T6 tube, with a locking adjustable dioptre; available in FDE and black finishes.
- **Reticle:** The RAR reticle is a simple, easy-to-use second-focal-plane design with built-in BDC calculations; illuminated with six brightness settings via a side dial with off positions between each level.
- **Controls:** Smooth magnification with a removable throw lever; capped elevation and windage turrets; 120 MOA total internal adjustment with 40 MOA per revolution in half-MOA clicks.
- **Optics:** 24mm objective with fully multi-coated glass and an IPX7 waterproof rating for harsh conditions.
- **In the box (as shown):** Bungee-style scope cap, warranty and information card, an Allen key for turret adjustments, and an accessory pack with reticle chart, stickers, and lens cloth.
- **Intended use:** An affordable option for close to intermediate range, well suited to AR platforms, brush guns, or lever actions — a lightweight, rugged package for tactical and general-purpose use.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance LPVO manual reference** (`_Vengeance_LPVO_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** 2mm hex key (turret centre bolt)
- **Turret index:** 0.5 MOA — approx. 15mm at 100 metres (0.5" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** RAR Illuminated — six brightness settings, CR2032 battery
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m — no adjustment
