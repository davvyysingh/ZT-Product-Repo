## Key features
- **PHR 2 Illuminated reticle (glass-etched, SFP)** — a floating centre dot and finer 6 o'clock post for refined aiming and precise elevation holdovers, with illumination for low-light use and unobtrusive elevation holds at multiple MOA intervals.
- **Large 56mm objective with HD glass and 92% light transmission** — bright, high-resolution images for dawn, dusk, and night work such as spotlighting.
- **3–18x magnification** — a wide field of view at 3x for fast acquisition and reach to 18x for long-range precision.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strength and strong light transmission.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 10 metres to infinity; half-magnification indicator (HMI) on the magnification ring marks the reticle's calibrated half-power.
- **Capped, resettable 0.25 MOA turrets** — precise, repeatable adjustments with easy return to zero.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Weathershield lens coating, IPX7 waterproof** — durability and clarity in changing weather.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech walkthrough of the Thrive HD 3-18x56:
- **Configuration:** Offered with illuminated and non-illuminated reticles in PHR2 and R3, all glass-etched and calibrated in MOA. Features side parallax, HD alloy flip-up covers, and capped, resettable elevation and windage turrets for easy return to zero. The magnification ring carries a half-magnification index matching the reticle's calibration at half power.
- **Purpose:** Developed as a large-objective, higher-magnification optic for low-light shooting such as spotlighting, and to suit European-style magnification ranges; reticles were designed from real shooting experience and practical distances.
- **Low-light performance:** As part of the Thrive HD series, the 56mm objective and ultra-clear HD glass deliver high resolution and a bright image at dawn, dusk, and night, while the 3x low end gives a wide field of view for fast acquisition in dense bush.
- **Backing:** Supported by ZeroTech's AAA unconditional lifetime warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** ZeroTech multi-tool — remove the turret centre bolt, re-index to '0'
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Push-button — 5 brightness settings + Off, CR2032 battery (3-hour auto-off, memory function)
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
