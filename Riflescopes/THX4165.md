# ZeroTech Thrive X 4-16x50 – ZEROPLEX (THX4165)

## Summary
A lightweight 30mm-tube, second-focal-plane 4–16×50 hunting scope with the traditional ZEROPLEX reticle, capped 0.25 MOA turrets with 60 MOA of travel, side parallax from 9.2 m and a 50mm objective for a generous exit pupil in low light — equally at home on a lightweight deer rifle or a heavy-barrel varmint rig, and upgrade-ready for the Adjustable BDC Turret.

## Key features
- **ZEROPLEX reticle** — mimics the feel of a traditional riflescope while the scope is packed with modern features; on this second-focal-plane design the reticle stays the same size at every power, with holds calibrated at 16x.
- **50mm objective** — forms an important part of the equation, giving an ideal exit pupil diameter at any magnification and improving comfort behind the scope, especially in low light.
- **4x to 16x with parallax from 9.2 m to infinity** — ultimate versatility in the lightest possible package, from a lightweight deer rifle to a heavy-barrel, high-volume varmint rig.
- **Capped 0.25 MOA turrets with 60 MOA of elevation and windage** — run capped or uncapped and hold the zero you set either way; tool-free coin-slot turret reset.
- **Upgrade-ready for the Adjustable BDC Turret** — sold separately; ZEROPLEX (MOA turret) Thrive X models are compatible.
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive X and Thrive HD models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **Same bones as the rest of the Thrive X range** — 30mm 6061-T6 aluminium maintube, fully multi-coated glass and an IPX7 waterproof rating, built and rigorously tested in real-world use; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty, transferable to the next owner with no paperwork.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive X** sections for the turret reset and windage limiter. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Capped elevation turret model — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); repeat for windage
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; remove the dial and reset to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **BDC upgrade:** Compatible with the Adjustable BDC Turret (sold separately); fit the thread protector ring when the BDC turret is in place
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 9.2m (10yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** Neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
