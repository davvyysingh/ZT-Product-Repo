# ZeroTech Thrive 4-16x50 – G4 (TH41650G)

A 30mm-tube, second-focal-plane 4–16×50 with the traditional-style G4 reticle, capped 0.25 MOA turrets, side parallax, and a large 50mm objective — a value-focused long-range and low-light hunting scope.

## Key features
- **G4 reticle** — a traditional-style reticle with a crisp floating centre dot for precise shot placement, while keeping a broad field of view and versatility across distances.
- **4–16x magnification with a large 50mm objective** — long-range reach with strong light gathering for bright, clear images in low light.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — rigidity, strength, and superior light transmission.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 10 metres to infinity.
- **Capped 0.25 MOA turrets** — precise, repeatable adjustments that stay secure in the field.
- **Locking dioptre** — set and hold a sharp reticle.
- **Weathershield lens coating with fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — protection against dust and moisture with a clear sight picture.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
