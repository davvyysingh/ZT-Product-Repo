## Key features
- **PHR SFP reticle** — developed and tested for high precision and fast target acquisition without needing to dial the turret.
- **4.5–18x magnification with 40mm objective** — a versatile range for scrub, bush, and rimfire shooting.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 15 metres to infinity.
- **Pop-Lok 0.25 MOA turrets** — repeatable, lockable adjustments.
- **1-inch 6061-T6 aluminium tube** — lightweight and balanced; fits standard rings.
- **Fast-focus dioptre** — quick reticle sharpening.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — dependable field performance.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
