## Key features
- **PHR 4 reticle** — bold outer posts for fast target acquisition, fine inner subtensions for precise holds and leads.
- **4–16x magnification range** — versatile from closer hunting scenarios to longer-distance shots.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — resolves parallax error from 15 metres to infinity for a sharp image at any distance.
- **Locking 0.25 MOA turrets with return-to-zero** — secure, repeatable adjustments that hold through recoil and rough travel.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick, controlled magnification changes, even with gloves.
- **1-inch 6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium tube** — lightweight handling with strong durability; fits standard rings.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses** — improved brightness and contrast in challenging light.
- **IPX7 waterproof**, tested to Australian standards for waterproofing and shock resistance.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Independent video reviews demonstrate the Vengeance 4-16x40 as a capable, no-nonsense hunting and range optic:
- **Real-world accuracy:** On a Savage Axis II Pro, the scope grouped tightly at 300 yards and — with custom turret markings based on ballistic data — dialled from 100 out to 600 yards. On a Lithgow L102 Varmint it held a consistent zero and successfully engaged targets at 200, 300, and 480 metres.
- **Handling features highlighted:** Reviewers single out the throw lever for quick magnification changes, locking turrets that reset easily via simple top and side bolts, and a smooth, user-friendly parallax adjustment, all in a second-focal-plane design with a practical hunting reticle.
- **Tracking and value:** Extensive testing confirms the turrets track accurately. Glass clarity is rated solid for the price point, with reviewers noting that locking turrets and reliable tracking — once reserved for far more expensive optics — are available here at an accessible price.
- **Intended use:** Positioned as a dependable, reliability-over-gimmicks optic primarily for 100–300 yard hunting, with enough capability to stretch further when needed. Available with the PHR 4 reticle in both illuminated and non-illuminated versions (this SKU is non-illuminated).
