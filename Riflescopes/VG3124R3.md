# ZeroTech Vengeance 3-12x40 R3 (VG3124R3)

## Summary
A lightweight 1-inch, second-focal-plane 3–12×40 with the glass-etched R3 reticle, locking 0.25 MOA turrets with zero reset, and side parallax — a clean, practical scrub and bush hunting scope.

## Key features
- **R3 reticle (glass-etched, SFP)** — floating centre dot, 1 MOA subtension markings, and hash marks every 4 MOA for consistent elevation and wind holds; bold cardinal reference bars guide the eye quickly to centre for fast target acquisition.
- **3–12x magnification range** — adaptable clarity from close shots to extended ranges.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — resolves parallax error from 15 metres to infinity.
- **Locking turrets with zero reset (0.25 MOA)** — reliable, repeatable adjustments.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick magnification changes when needed.
- **1-inch aircraft-grade aluminium tube** — lightweight, classic-ring compatible, sealed and ruggedised.
- **Fully multi-coated optics, IPX7 waterproof** — crisp contrast and bright images in all light.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
