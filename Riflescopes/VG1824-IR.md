## Key features
- **RAR (Rapid Acquisition Reticle), SFP Illuminated** — bold outer bars guide the eye to an illuminated three-quarter circle and centre dot for fast transitions, with 2 MOA hash marks and numbered 4 MOA elevation intervals for wind and elevation holds.
- **True 1× to 8× magnification** — both-eyes-open speed up close, with controlled precision well beyond 200 metres at 8×.
- **24mm objective with 30mm 6061-T6 aircraft-grade tube** — strength, stability, and a clean, bright sight picture.
- **0.25 MOA capped turrets** — protected adjustments with 120 MOA total internal travel.
- **Locking dioptre** — secure the setting for a repeatable, crisp reticle.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — clarity and reliable sealing in demanding conditions.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's AAA Lifetime Warranty.

## Video insights
Two video reviews — a ZeroTech walkthrough (Mac from ZeroTech) and an independent review — cover the Vengeance 1-8x24 LPVO:
- **Build:** 30mm 6061-T6 tube, available in FDE and black anodised finishes; 24mm fully multi-coated objective; locking adjustable dioptre that stays set once adjusted.
- **Reticle and illumination:** The RAR reticle (SFP, constant size across magnification) uses a bold centre dot and clear stadia lines for quick, intuitive acquisition. Illumination has six brightness settings with off positions between each, is daylight-bright even in harsh sun, and is night-vision compatible; the battery housing is separate from the illumination dial. Runs on a CR2032 (remove any protective film on install).
- **Turrets:** Quarter-MOA (0.25 MOA) adjustments with low-profile capped windage/elevation turrets; 120 MOA total internal travel and 24 MOA per revolution.
- **Optics and handling:** Wide field of view of around 107 ft at 1× with a comfortable, forgiving eyebox and minimal distortion; sharpness and accurate colour hold through mid magnification, and 8× retains strong clarity for longer shots. The magnification ring is smooth with the included throw lever.
- **Performance:** A versatile balance of magnification and usability — effective from close range out to several hundred yards (up to 700–800 yards with the right setup), suited to AR platforms, lever actions, and lightweight hunting rifles.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Vengeance LPVO manual reference** (`_Vengeance_LPVO_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** 2mm hex key (turret centre bolt)
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** RAR Illuminated — six brightness settings, CR2032 battery
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m — no adjustment
