## Key features
- **First Focal Plane (FFP) reticle** — reticle size scales with magnification, maintaining constant elevation and windage holds at any distance and any magnification power.
- **ZeroStop turrets** — reliable return to zero after dialling elevation and windage corrections, giving a quick field reference.
- **RMG MOA reticle** — MOA reticle and adjustments for precise targeting without constantly dialling.
- **Extensive internal adjustment** — 80 MOA elevation and 68 MOA windage.
- **44mm objective with 30mm one-piece maintube** — strong light transmission and mounting rigidity.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — quick, controlled magnification changes.
- **6061-T6 maintube, FMC lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — durable and weather-sealed.
- **0.25 MOA turret index.**
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent video review mounts the Vengeance 4-16x44 FFP (MOA configuration) on an LFA Hunter .308:
- **Build and controls:** Smooth magnification adjustment with the included removable throw lever; zero-stop turrets with tactile, slightly quiet but very noticeable clicks; adjustable dioptre at the rear and a side focus/parallax adjustment. The 30mm tube and 44mm objective give solid light transmission. The reviewer confirms both the reticle and adjustments are in MOA.
- **Performance:** At 100 yards it performs very well, easily hitting smaller targets. The reviewer notes a field of view of 8.8 metres at 4x and 2.2 metres at 16x, and a weight of around 23 ounces — relatively lightweight while still gathering plenty of light.
- **Low light:** Works well in low-light conditions such as dawn or dusk, making it a solid hunting option.
- **Glass quality:** Described as extremely clear, with the reviewer rating overall ZeroTech build quality as impressive and the scope a reliable, high-performing choice.
