# ZeroTech Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for ZeroTech Thrive-family riflescopes. Individual SKU files link here and note which model-specific sections apply (turret index/click value, illumination, lens covers). Three ZeroTech manuals are combined here: the 2022 Thrive / Thrive HD manual, the 2026 Thrive X manual and the 2026 Thrive HD manual (Adjustable BDC Turret models). Where a procedure differs between them, this file gives each its own clearly labelled section — check the model group below before following a procedure. (The Thrive / Thrive HD manual document also covers Trace models; Trace turret mechanics — locking turrets and the grub-screw zero stop — are kept in the separate Trace manual reference.)

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty (all models)
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge. (Does not cover intentional loss or damage.)

## Setting the dioptre (all models — do this first)
Set magnification to maximum and, if fitted, the parallax turret to the infinity marker. Look briefly (no more than 5 seconds at a time) at a blank, featureless and bright surface such as a white wall or clear blue sky — never look directly at the sun. If the reticle isn't immediately sharp and clear, twist the dioptre adjuster until it is; the dioptre adjuster has approximately 1¾ turns of adjustment. ZeroTech recommends starting with the dioptre flush against the ocular and adjusting anticlockwise (toward the + symbol) until optimal. Note the setting for future reference.

## Mounting (all models)
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present. ZeroTech recommends professional mounting to preserve the warranty. Use quality rings/bases; with ring tops removed, confirm the scope slides freely (lap rings if needed); avoid rings contacting the maintube/erector, magnification-ring or objective junctions; confirm no part of the scope touches the barrel or receiver. A bubble level is highly recommended for consistent reticle alignment, especially for precision shooting in uneven or steep terrain; a level fitted to the maintube follows the same fitting guidelines as rings.

Ring-top torque as stated in each manual:
- **Thrive / Thrive HD:** 22 – 25 in lbs (2.5 – 2.8 NM).
- **Thrive X and Thrive HD Adjustable BDC Turret models (THDB):** as specified by the mount manufacturer, normally 18 – 25 in lbs.

## Adjusting parallax (side focus — all models)
If fitted with a parallax turret, with the rifle well supported, use the parallax turret to obtain the sharpest target image and a refined point of aim. Move your head gently up/down — if the reticle drifts from the aim point, adjust the turret until it stays fixed. Repeat for different distances and conditions; the distance markings are a guide only.

## Zeroing (all models)
ZeroTech recommends zeroing most centrefire rifles at approximately 100 metres +/- 10 metres; rimfire hunting rifles are most commonly zeroed at 50 – 75 metres depending on the ammunition. Zero from the position you normally shoot. Bore sight first. Dialling the elevation turret toward the **'U'** symbol moves impact up; the windage turret toward the **'R'** symbol moves it right.

Click values per the manuals:
- **MOA turrets:** ¼ Minutes of Angle (0.25 MOA) adjust bullet impact approximately 7-8mm at 100 metres (approx. ¼" at 100 yards) per click.
- **MRAD turrets:** 0.1 Milrad (0.1 MRAD) adjust bullet impact 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click.

## Resetting the turrets after zeroing

### Thrive & Thrive HD — multi-tool centre-bolt turrets (TH… SKUs, 2022 manual)
Use the ZeroTech multi-tool (or a coin). Holding the turret firmly, insert the multi-tool into the slot in the turret centre bolt and unscrew it anticlockwise to remove the centre bolt; lift the turret away from the scope. Refit the turret with the '0' marking aligned to the index line on the scope body, then hold the turret firmly and tighten the centre bolt clockwise. Repeat for the windage turret, then replace the turret caps so the zero settings aren't disturbed. (It is not necessary to overtighten.)

### Thrive X — models with capped elevation turret (ZEROPLEX and Mildot reticles)
Holding the turret dial in place, use a small coin to unscrew the turret keeper anticlockwise; remove the keeper and the turret dial. Replace the dial with the reference set to '0', then refit and tighten the keeper with the coin — only light pressure is needed; do not use a screwdriver. Check that the '0' on the dial aligns with the index mark on the turret body. Repeat for the windage turret, then replace the turret caps so the zero settings aren't disturbed.

### Thrive X and Thrive HD — models with the Adjustable BDC elevation turret (THXB and THDB)
Reset the BDC elevation turret the same way: coin off the keeper, lift the dial, replace at '0', refit the keeper with light pressure, check the alignment, repeat for the windage turret and replace the caps. If the index marker on the inner turret body and the markings on the BDC turret are slightly misaligned: remove the top screw with a small coin; beneath it are three evenly spaced screws — loosen each ¼ to ½ turn with a 1.5mm hex key so the turret spins freely (without clicks); align the index marks; then gently tighten the three screws using the short end of the key between thumb and index finger. Do not overtighten. A thread protector ring must be fitted on the elevation turret when the BDC turret is in place.

**Setting the Adjustable BDC elevation turret (THXB and THDB models):** once the rifle is zeroed, twist the Adjustable BDC dial on top of the elevation turret from the locked position to the number of the corresponding BDC marker (only the number 1 marker is adjustable in the unlocked position shown in the manual), rotate the marker to the desired position, then twist the dial back to the locked position.

## Windage turret rotation limiter (Thrive X and Thrive HD)
Thrive X and Thrive HD windage turret dials are fitted with a limiter pin on the underside of the dial, which limits the windage dial to about half a turn — 7 MOA either side of the '0' position — so the turret cannot be run from "left" into "right" without noticing. If you hit the limiter but still need more adjustment, remove the windage turret dial and reset it to '0'; the full range of windage travel is then available again. If you reach the limiter while zeroing before you have achieved zero, you have not run out of windage travel — reset the windage dial to '0', make the additional adjustment, then reset the dial around the new zero so you again have 7 MOA of travel either side. To remove the limiter entirely: unscrew the thread cap, remove the locking screw and lift off the windage turret dial; the limiter pin is on the underside of the dial and can be removed with a small flat-head screwdriver. Once removed, the pin cannot be refitted.

## Reticle illumination (Thrive HD illuminated models — push button)
Illuminated Thrive HD models, including the 2026 Adjustable BDC Turret models (THDB2155P-IR, THDB6245P-IR), use a push-button illumination function with 6 settings (5 illumination settings + an Off setting); on the 2026 models the button sits on the parallax turret with a screw-on battery cover. Before use, install the CR2032 battery by unscrewing the illumination turret battery cover anticlockwise. Press the control button once to illuminate the reticle, and press quickly to toggle brightness. To turn illumination off, press and hold the button for 2 – 3 seconds. The illumination auto-powers-off after 3 hours to conserve the battery, and a memory function resumes on the last-used brightness setting. Carry a spare CR2032 when relying on illumination. Thrive X 3-9x40, 3-12x44 and 4-16x50 models are not illuminated.

## Battery safety warning (illuminated models)
Illuminated models contain a CR2032 3V coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. Install correctly per polarity; do not mix old/new or different battery types; do not recharge, force-discharge, disassemble, heat or incinerate. Always secure the battery compartment; if it will not close securely, stop using the product and remove the battery. Recycle or dispose of used batteries according to local regulations, never in household rubbish. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) or your local poison control centre immediately.

## Lens covers and multi-tool
- **Thrive HD:** fitted with removable HD alloy flip-up lens covers that can be rotated to the shooter's preferred position (and removed) using the ZeroTech multi-tool. The Thrive-series multi-tool also resets the elevation and windage turrets and adjusts the lens-cover lock rings.
- **Thrive X:** supplied with a neoprene stretch-fit scope cover; turrets reset with a coin, no multi-tool required. The magnification ring has a throw-lever dovetail for the removable throw lever.
- **Thrive HD Adjustable BDC Turret models (THDB):** fitted with heavy-duty aluminium flip-up lens covers and supplied with a neoprene stretch-fit scope cover; turrets reset with a coin; throw-lever dovetail on the magnification ring.

## Scope care (all models)
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth.
