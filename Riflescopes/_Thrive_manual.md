# ZeroTech Thrive / Thrive HD — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for ZeroTech Thrive and Thrive HD riflescopes. Individual SKU files link here and note which model-specific sections apply (turret index/click value, illumination, lens covers). (This manual document also covers Trace models; Trace turret mechanics — locking turrets and the grub-screw zero stop — are kept in the separate Trace manual reference.)

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Setting the dioptre (do this first)
Set magnification to maximum and, if fitted, the parallax turret to the infinity marker. Look briefly (no more than 5 seconds at a time) at a blank, featureless and bright surface such as a white wall or clear blue sky — never look directly at the sun. If the reticle isn't immediately sharp and clear, twist the dioptre adjuster until it is; the dioptre adjuster has approximately 1¾ turns of adjustment. ZeroTech recommends starting with the dioptre flush against the ocular and adjusting anticlockwise (toward the + symbol) until optimal. Note the setting for future reference.

## Mounting
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present. ZeroTech recommends professional mounting to preserve the warranty. Use quality rings/bases; with ring tops removed, confirm the scope slides freely (lap rings if needed); avoid rings contacting the maintube/erector, magnification-ring or objective junctions; confirm no part of the scope touches the barrel or receiver. Tighten ring tops to between 22 – 25 in lbs (2.5 – 2.8 NM). A bubble level is highly recommended for consistent reticle alignment.

## Adjusting parallax (side focus)
If fitted with a parallax turret, with the rifle well supported, use the parallax turret to obtain the sharpest target image and a refined point of aim. Move your head gently up/down — if the reticle drifts from the aim point, adjust the turret until it stays fixed. Repeat for different distances; the distance markings are a guide only.

## Zeroing
ZeroTech recommends zeroing most centrefire rifles at approximately 100 metres +/- 10 metres; rimfire hunting rifles are most commonly zeroed at 50 – 75 metres depending on the ammunition. Bore sight first. Dialling the elevation turret toward the **'U'** symbol moves impact up; the windage turret toward the **'R'** symbol moves it right.

Click values per the manual:
- **MOA turrets:** ¼ Minutes of Angle (0.25 MOA) adjust bullet impact approximately 7-8mm at 100 metres (approx. ¼" at 100 yards) per click.
- **MRAD turrets:** 0.1 Milrad (0.1 MRAD) adjust bullet impact 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click.

## Resetting the turrets after zeroing (Thrive & Thrive HD)
Use the ZeroTech multi-tool (or a coin). Holding the turret firmly, insert the multi-tool into the slot in the turret centre bolt and unscrew it anticlockwise to remove the centre bolt; lift the turret away from the scope. Refit the turret with the '0' marking aligned to the index line on the scope body, then hold the turret firmly and tighten the centre bolt clockwise. Repeat for the windage turret, then replace the turret caps so the zero settings aren't disturbed. (It is not necessary to overtighten.)

## Reticle illumination (illuminated models — push button)
Illuminated Thrive HD models use a push-button illumination function with 6 settings (5 illumination settings + an Off setting). Before use, install the CR2032 battery by unscrewing the illumination turret battery cover anticlockwise. Press the control button once to illuminate the reticle, and press quickly to toggle brightness. To turn illumination off, press and hold the button for 2 – 3 seconds. The illumination auto-powers-off after 3 hours to conserve the battery, and a memory function resumes on the last-used brightness setting. Carry a spare CR2032 when relying on illumination.

## Battery safety warning (illuminated models)
Illuminated models contain a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Lens covers and multi-tool
Thrive HD models are fitted with removable HD alloy flip-up lens covers that can be rotated to the shooter's preferred position (and removed) using the ZeroTech multi-tool. The Thrive-series multi-tool also resets the elevation and windage turrets and adjusts the lens-cover lock rings.

## Scope care
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth.
