## Key features
- **PHR 4 Illuminated reticle** — bold outer posts guide the eye to centre, fine inner subtensions for precise aiming, with six brightness settings to stay visible in fading light without overwhelming the target.
- **4–16x magnification range** — versatile from tight bush terrain to long open shots.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — resolves parallax error from 15 metres to infinity.
- **Locking 0.25 MOA turrets with return-to-zero** — repeatable adjustments with reliable tracking.
- **Removable magnification throw lever** — smooth, controlled magnification changes.
- **1-inch 6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium tube** — lightweight, balanced, and waterproof, fogproof and shockproof.

## Video insights
Independent video reviews of the Vengeance 4-16x40 (the reviewers note it's offered in both illuminated and non-illuminated PHR 4 versions):
- **Real-world accuracy:** On a Savage Axis II Pro, the scope grouped tightly at 300 yards and — with custom turret markings based on ballistic data — dialled from 100 out to 600 yards. On a Lithgow L102 Varmint it held a consistent zero and successfully engaged targets at 200, 300, and 480 metres.
- **Handling features highlighted:** Reviewers single out the throw lever for quick magnification changes, locking turrets that reset easily via simple top and side bolts, and a smooth, user-friendly parallax adjustment, all in a second-focal-plane design with a practical hunting reticle.
- **Tracking and value:** Extensive testing confirms the turrets track accurately. Glass clarity is rated solid for the price point, with reviewers noting locking turrets and reliable tracking once reserved for far more expensive optics.
- **Intended use:** Positioned as a dependable, reliability-over-gimmicks optic primarily for 100–300 yard hunting, with capability to stretch further when needed.
