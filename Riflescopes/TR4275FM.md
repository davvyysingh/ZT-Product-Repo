## Key features
- **FFP RMG MOA reticle (glass-etched)** — the MOA-calibrated version of the Refined Mil Grid, built for precision and speed with MOA increments and measuring bars for accurate elevation and windage holds; a floating centre dot for a refined aiming point and heavy bars with chevron ends at the 3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock positions to draw the eye to centre at lower magnifications. The MRAD RMG is also available.
- **4.5–27x magnification with 50mm objective and ~92% light transmission** — a wide range for competition, PRS, and backcountry hunting with bright, clear images.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strength and superior light transmission, with Weathershield lens coatings.
- **Locking turrets with zero stop** — precise, repeatable adjustments (0.25 MOA, up to 60 MOA) and a reliable return to zero; lift to adjust, press to lock.
- **Side parallax (25 m to infinity)** — smooth focus across distances.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Reviews of the Trace 4.5-27x50 FFP platform (this SKU uses the MOA-calibrated RMG):
- **Build and package:** A solid, slightly heavy one-piece 30mm tube reflecting durability, with included alloy flip-up lens caps, a turret multi-tool, an Allen key, and spare grub screws; manufactured in China to rugged Australian standards.
- **Turrets and tracking:** Firm, smooth turrets with crisp, audible clicks; a locking system (lift to unlock, press to lock) prevents accidental changes; an easy-to-set, precisely machined zero stop. Tracking tested stop-to-stop returned to zero consistently with no point-of-impact shift.
- **Reticle:** The clean, glass-etched RMG grid has a fine floating centre dot, clearly labelled references, and thicker outer stadia for acquisition at low magnification — efficient for holdovers; offered in MOA (this SKU) and MRAD.
- **Optics:** Around 90–92% light transmission gives good low-light brightness and solid clarity to 27×, with comfortable 91–100 mm eye relief suited to shooters wearing glasses.
- **Verdict:** Reliable, repeatable, and durable under demanding testing, delivering standout value for its price with a transferable lifetime warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); locking windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 25m (27yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
