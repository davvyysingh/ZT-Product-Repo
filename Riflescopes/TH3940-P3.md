## Key features
- **PHR 3 SFP reticle** — a modern take on the classic crosshair that incorporates benefits of the PHR II reticle in a simplified format, supporting precise long-range aiming while still allowing fast snap shots on moving targets.
- **3–9x magnification with 40mm objective** — a versatile range for scrub, bush, low-light, and rimfire shooting.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strength and excellent light transmission for bright, clear images.
- **Capped 0.25 MOA turrets** — precise, repeatable adjustments that stay secure in the field.
- **Locking dioptre** — set and hold a sharp reticle.
- **Weathershield lens coating with fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — protection against dust and moisture with a clear sight picture.
- **Non-illuminated**, fixed parallax; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
