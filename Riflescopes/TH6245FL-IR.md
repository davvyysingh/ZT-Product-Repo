## Key features
- **FFP LR Hunter Illuminated reticle (glass-etched)** — a hybrid of precision, simplicity, and speed: at low magnification it mimics traditional reticle designs for fast target acquisition, and at higher magnification it offers the precision needed for distant targets; adjustable illumination improves visibility across lighting conditions.
- **First Focal Plane** — reticle subtensions stay true at every magnification for consistent holds.
- **6–24x magnification with 50mm objective and HD glass (92% light transmission)** — high magnification and strong light gathering for extended range and low light.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — rigidity and enhanced light transmission.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 10 metres to infinity.
- **Capped 0.1 MRAD turrets** — precise, repeatable adjustments that stay secure in the field.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Weathershield lens coating, IPX7 waterproof** — durability and clarity in changing weather.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
