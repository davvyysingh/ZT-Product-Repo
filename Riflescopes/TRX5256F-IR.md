## Key features
- **Illuminated RMG-H first-focal-plane reticle (MRAD)** — holds every subtension true from 5x to 25x, so wind and elevation holds stay consistent across the magnification range; illumination enhances visibility on lower power and against dark backgrounds.
- **0.1 MRAD turrets with zero stop** — an exposed elevation turret and capped windage; the zero stop sets a hard floor at your zero so a return from any dialled elevation lands where it should.
- **34mm 6061-T6 one-piece tube with 34 MRAD of elevation and 17 MRAD of windage** — generous internal travel for long-range work on most shooting systems.
- **5–25x magnification with a 56mm objective** — a crisp optical system that includes a premium German Schott glass element for refined clarity and image detail, with fully multi-coated lenses.
- **Side parallax from 20 m to infinity, lockable dioptre and a removable two-position throw lever** — fine-tune focus from close range out, lock the reticle focus, and set the lever where it suits your bolt throw.
- **Push-button reticle illumination** — enhances reticle visibility, especially at lower magnification settings.
- **IPX7 waterproof** — built to endure all conditions; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk walks through the Trace X 5-25x56 as a blend of long-range precision scope and hunting optic:
- **Layout:** 34mm tube with an open-top elevation turret, capped windage, side-button illumination on the parallax turret, adjustable parallax and a lockable dioptre.
- **Throw lever:** A removable, two-position throw lever stud that is deliberately not oversized, so a 90° bolt throw is less likely to catch your thumb; the presenter runs it at the 16x position for extra clearance, with the alternative position for shooters who prefer the traditional angle or have a shorter bolt throw.
- **Glass and build:** 56mm fully multi-coated objective for light transmission, glare reduction and scratch resistance; aircraft-grade 6061-T6 tube; IPX7, so the scope can be fully submerged.
- **Illumination:** Push button on the parallax side — hold to switch off, press once to return to the previous brightness; settings step upward, and at the top the reticle blinks and drops back to the lowest setting.
- **Turrets:** First focal plane with the RMG, RMG MOA and RMG 3 reticles. MRAD versions give 34 mil of elevation and 17 mil of windage in 0.1 mil clicks, 10 mil per revolution, with audible, tactile clicks you can count without leaving the glass; the MOA version gives 116 MOA elevation and 58 MOA windage in ¼ MOA clicks, 24 MOA per revolution. The elevation cap comes off via two pins, with the zero stop underneath.
- **In the box:** Neoprene scope cover, rubberised bungee lens caps, hex keys for the turret and zero stop, stickers, the QR card and a lens cloth.
- **Overall:** The presenter rates the Trace X line highly for look, feel and performance, with magnification options to suit hunting, competition and target shooting.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced / Trace X manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Trace X** sections for the zero stop, windage reset and illumination. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Remove the elevation turret via its two 2mm grub screws, set the orange zero-stop ring with its three 1.5mm grub screws (pin on the right of the lower pin), refit the turret at '0'; capped windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its two 2mm grub screws without removing the cap
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click (10 mils per revolution)
- **Illumination:** Push-button digital on the parallax turret — 6 brightness settings, CR2032 battery (blinks twice at maximum; hold 2–3s to switch off; ~2-hour auto-off, resumes previous setting)
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 20m (22yd) to infinity
- **Dioptre:** -3 to +2, approximately 1¾ turns; turn the lock ring clockwise to secure the setting
