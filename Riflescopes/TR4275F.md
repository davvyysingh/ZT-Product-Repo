## Key features
- **FFP RMG (Refined Mil Grid) reticle (glass-etched)** — built for precision and speed with 0.5 mil increments and integrated 0.1 mil measuring bars for accurate elevation and windage holds; a floating centre dot for a refined aiming point and heavy bars with chevron ends at the 3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock positions to draw the eye to centre at lower magnifications. Also available in RMG MOA and R3.
- **4.5–27x magnification with 50mm objective and 92% light transmission** — a wide range for competition, PRS, and backcountry hunting with bright, clear images.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strength and superior light transmission, with Weathershield lens coatings.
- **Precision Turret System (PTS) with zero stop** — precise, repeatable adjustments (0.1 MRAD) and a reliable return to zero; turrets lock by pressing down and unlock by lifting.
- **Side parallax (25 m to infinity)** — smooth focus across distances.
- **HD alloy flip-up lens covers** — adjustable and removable for protection.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Multiple independent reviews test the Trace 4.5-27x50 FFP RMG (some refer to it as "Trace Advanced") as a feature-rich, well-built precision scope:
- **Build and package:** A solid, slightly heavy one-piece 30mm tube reflecting durability, with included alloy flip-up lens caps (often an added cost elsewhere), a turret multi-tool, an Allen key, and spare grub screws. Reviewers noted it's manufactured in China and designed to rugged Australian standards.
- **Turrets and tracking:** Firm, smooth turrets with crisp, audible clicks and clear numbering; a locking system (lift to unlock, press to lock) prevents accidental changes; an easy-to-set, precisely machined zero stop. Tracking tested stop-to-stop returned to zero consistently with no point-of-impact shift.
- **Reticle:** The clean, glass-etched RMG grid has a fine floating centre dot, clearly labelled mil references, half-mil indicators, and thicker outer stadia for acquisition at low magnification — efficient for holdovers and available in MRAD and MOA.
- **Optics:** Around 90–92% light transmission on the 50mm objective gives good low-light brightness and solid clarity to 27× (brightness eases slightly at maximum magnification); comfortable 91–100 mm eye relief suits shooters wearing glasses.
- **Real-world use:** Run on rifles including a .308 Ruger Precision Rifle, .30-06, and a CZ .22 (holding zero through remounting), reviewers rated it reliable, repeatable, and excellent value with a transferable lifetime warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); locking windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 25m (27yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** HD alloy flip-up covers (adjustable/removable via the locking-ring tool)
