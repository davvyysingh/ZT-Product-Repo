# ZeroTech Trace / Trace Advanced / Trace X — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for ZeroTech Trace-family riflescopes. Individual SKU files link here and note which model-specific sections apply (turret index/click value, illumination type, parallax, lens covers). Two ZeroTech manuals are combined here: the Trace / Trace Advanced manual and the Trace X manual. Where a procedure differs between them, this file gives each its own clearly labelled section — check the model group below before following a procedure.

## Setting the dioptre (all models — do this first)
Set magnification to maximum and, if fitted, the parallax turret to the infinity marker. Look briefly (no more than 5 seconds at a time) at a blank, featureless and bright surface such as a white wall or clear blue sky — never look directly at the sun. If the reticle isn't immediately sharp and clear, twist the dioptre adjuster until it is; the adjuster has approximately 1¾ turns of travel. ZeroTech recommends starting flush against the ocular and adjusting anticlockwise (toward the + symbol) until optimal. Note the setting for future reference.

**Locking the dioptre:** where a dioptre lock ring is fitted (the Trace / Trace Advanced manual names the 5-30x56 model; all Trace X 5-25x56 models have one), turn the lock ring clockwise after setting the dioptre to secure the setting.

## Mounting (all models)
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present. ZeroTech recommends professional mounting to preserve the warranty. Use quality rings/bases; with ring tops removed, confirm the scope slides freely (lap rings if needed); avoid rings contacting the maintube/erector, magnification-ring or objective junctions; confirm no part of the scope touches the barrel or receiver. A bubble level is highly recommended, especially for precision shooting in uneven or steep terrain; a level fitted to the maintube follows the same fitting guidelines as rings.

Ring-top torque as stated in each manual:
- **Trace / Trace Advanced:** 22 – 25 in lbs (2.5 – 2.8 NM).
- **Trace X:** as specified by the mount manufacturer, normally 18 – 25 in lbs.

## Adjusting parallax (side focus — all models)
If fitted with a parallax turret, with the rifle well supported, use the parallax turret to obtain the sharpest target image and a refined point of aim. Move your head gently up/down — if the reticle drifts from the aim point, adjust the turret until it stays fixed. Repeat for different distances and conditions; the distance markings are a guide only.

## Zeroing (all models)
ZeroTech recommends zeroing most centrefire rifles at approximately 100 metres +/- 10 metres; rimfire hunting rifles are most commonly zeroed at 50 – 75 metres depending on ammunition. Zero from the position you normally shoot. Bore sight first. Dialling the elevation turret toward the **'U'** symbol moves impact up; the windage turret toward the **'R'** symbol moves it right.

Click values per the manuals:
- **MOA turrets:** ¼ Minutes of Angle (0.25 MOA) adjust bullet impact approximately 7-8mm at 100 metres (approx. ¼" at 100 yards) per click.
- **MRAD turrets:** 0.1 Milrad (0.1 MRAD) adjust bullet impact 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click.

## Setting the elevation zero stop

### Trace / Trace Advanced (4-24x50, 5-30x56, 3-18x44)
Once a zero is established, set the zero stop for a consistent return to zero after dialling:
1. Loosen the three (3) opposing grub screws in the top of the elevation turret with a 2mm hex key (4–5 revolutions each), remove the elevation turret, and set it aside.
2. With the turret removed, the orange zero-stop ring is visible. Loosen its three (3) opposing 2mm grub screws 1–2 revolutions each so the ring spins freely.
3. Slide the orange zero-stop ring downwards until it stops. Note the small pins on the underside of the zero-stop ring and the top of the lower turret assembly — the screw on the underside of the zero stop must sit to the right-hand side of the screw on the lower assembly (otherwise elevation will lock out until reset).
4. Tighten the three 2mm grub screws in the zero-stop ring.
5. Replace the elevation turret with the '0' aligned to the vertical index marker on the lower turret assembly and tighten the three 2mm grub screws (use the short end of the hex key for adequate torque).

### Trace X (5-25x56)
Once a zero is established, set the zero stop for a consistent return to zero after dialling:
1. Loosen the two (2) opposing grub screws in the top of the elevation turret with a 2mm hex key (4–5 revolutions each), remove the elevation turret, and set it aside.
2. With the turret removed, the orange zero-stop ring is visible. Loosen its three (3) opposing grub screws 1–2 revolutions each with a 1.5mm hex key so the ring spins freely.
3. Slide the orange zero-stop ring downwards until it stops. Note the small pins on the underside of the zero-stop ring and the top of the lower turret assembly — the pin on the underside of the zero stop must sit to the right-hand side of the pin on the lower assembly (otherwise elevation will lock out until the zero stop is reset).
4. Tighten the three 1.5mm grub screws in the zero-stop ring.
5. Replace the elevation turret with the '0' aligned to the vertical index marker on the lower turret assembly and tighten the two 2mm grub screws (use the short end of the hex key for adequate torque).

## Zeroing the windage turret
The windage turret has no zero stop on any Trace-family scope (it must adjust left and right equally).

### Trace / Trace Advanced (4-24x50, 5-30x56, 3-18x44)
With the turret unlocked, loosen the three (3) 2mm grub screws ½–1 revolution each so it spins freely, align the '0' with the index line on the lower turret assembly, then tighten the three grub screws.

### Trace X (5-25x56)
The capped windage turret can be reset without removing the cap. With the turret unlocked, loosen the two (2) 2mm grub screws ½–1 revolution each so it spins freely, align the '0' with the index line on the lower turret assembly, then tighten the two grub screws using the short end of the hex key.

## Reticle illumination
Install the CR2032 battery before use (unscrew the battery cover on the illumination turret anticlockwise; if pre-installed, remove the plastic circuit blocker and reinstall). Illumination type depends on the model:
- **Trace Advanced 4-24x50 models — mechanical dial:** 10 brightness settings with an "Off" position between each setting. Switch to "Off" for storage or transport.
- **Trace Advanced 5-30x56 & 3-18x44 models — push-button digital, 10 settings:** briefly press the button on the end of the parallax turret to activate; each press steps brightness up (the reticle blinks twice at maximum, then subsequent presses step down). Press and hold 2–3 seconds to switch off. If not switched off, illumination auto-shuts down after approximately 2 hours; a brief press resumes the previous setting.
- **Trace X 5-25x56 models — push-button digital, 6 settings:** briefly press the button on the end of the parallax turret to activate; each press steps brightness up one level (the illuminated parts of the reticle blink twice at maximum, then subsequent presses step down). Press and hold 2–3 seconds to switch off. If not switched off, illumination auto-shuts down after approximately 2 hours; a brief press resumes the previous setting.

Carry a spare CR2032 when relying on illumination, and use quality battery brands.

## Battery safety warning (illuminated models)
Illuminated models contain a CR2032 3V coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. Install correctly per polarity; do not mix old/new or different battery types; do not recharge, force-discharge, disassemble, heat or incinerate. Always secure the battery compartment; if it will not close securely, stop using the product and remove the battery. Recycle or dispose of used batteries according to local regulations, never in household rubbish. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) or your local poison control centre immediately.

## Lens covers
- **Trace / Trace Advanced (Tenebraex):** the 5-30x56 model is fitted with Tenebraex flip-up lens covers (this excludes the 3-18x44 model). Where fitted, they protect the lenses and flip clear for use.
- **Trace X 5-25x56:** supplied with a rubber bikini-style lens cover and a neoprene scope cover; no flip-up covers.

## Scope care (all models)
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth.
