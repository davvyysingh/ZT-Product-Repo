# ZeroTech Vengeance 6-24x50 R3 Illuminated (VG6245R3-IR)

## Key features
- **Glass-etched R3 Illuminated reticle** — fine hold points that stay crisp and consistent across the magnification range, with illumination to improve visibility in low light; clean and uncluttered for confident elevation and wind holds.
- **6–24x magnification with 50mm objective** — confident long-range target identification with a bright, detailed image in low light.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — corrects parallax from 15 metres to infinity for consistent shot placement.
- **1-inch tube** — lightweight and well balanced, pairing easily with standard ring systems.
- **0.25 MOA turret adjustments** — ZeroTech's trusted mechanical precision.
- **Fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — crisp contrast and brightness from dawn to the last shot of the day.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.
