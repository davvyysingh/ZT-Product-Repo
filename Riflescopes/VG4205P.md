## Key features
- **PHR 2 reticle** — refined floating centre dot with graduated elevation holds for precise shot placement without cluttering the sight picture; effective across the full magnification range.
- **4–20x magnification with 50mm objective** — bright, clear images and strong detail across a wide range of distances.
- **30mm one-piece tube** — strength, stability, and ample internal adjustment.
- **Pop-Lok 0.25 MOA turrets** — consistent, tactile feedback.
- **Fast-focus dioptre** — quick reticle adjustment in the field.
- **Fully multi-coated optics, IPX7 waterproof** — reliable performance in varied conditions.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent video review covers the Vengeance 4-20x50 as a budget-friendly yet feature-rich optic for hunting and general shooting:
- **Build and packaging:** Built on a 30mm tube with a premium-looking finish and durable coating; well-packaged with a scope cover, manual, lens cloth, and tools. The reviewer's unit was the MOA configuration with the PHR2 illuminated reticle (hunting-focused with useful holdover references).
- **Controls:** Side parallax adjustment, locking windage and elevation turrets, a smooth easy-to-use dioptre, and a raised magnification knob for improved handling. Turrets are resettable via a simple single Allen-screw system.
- **Versatility:** The 4x–20x range suits both close and extended distances, from brush country to longer-range shooting.
- **Illumination (illuminated variant):** Six brightness levels with off positions between each, though the reviewer felt it suited daylight use and could appear slightly bright in low light. (This SKU is the non-illuminated PHR 2.)
- **Optical quality:** Clear glass and reliable performance for the price; proved capable in low-light hunting with accurate shot placement.
