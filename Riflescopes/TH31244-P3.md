# ZeroTech Thrive 3-12x44 – PHR 3 (TH31244-P3)

## Key features
- **PHR 3 SFP reticle** — a modern take on the classic crosshair that incorporates benefits of the PHR II reticle in a simplified format, supporting precise long-range aiming while still allowing fast snap shots on moving targets.
- **3–12x magnification with 44mm objective** — a versatile range for scrub, bush, low-light, and rimfire shooting.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — strength and excellent light transmission for bright, clear images.
- **Side parallax adjustment** — sharp focus from 10 metres to infinity.
- **Capped 0.25 MOA turrets** — precise, repeatable adjustments that stay secure in the field.
- **Locking dioptre** — set and hold a sharp reticle.
- **Weathershield lens coating with fully multi-coated lenses, IPX7 waterproof** — protection against dust and moisture with a clear sight picture.
- **Non-illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent review of the Thrive 3-12x44 platform (reviewer's unit was the ZEROPLEX variant):
- **Purpose-built simplicity:** A straightforward hunting scope on a 30mm tube — reliability without unnecessary complexity, paired in the review with a Savage 110 in .270.
- **Controls:** Side parallax from 10 metres to infinity, an adjustable dioptre, and an intentionally stiff magnification ring to prevent accidental movement. Capped windage and elevation turrets adjust with a coin in quarter-MOA clicks and are tactile and audible — useful for hunting, where exposed dials can get bumped.
- **Glass:** In live shooting at 100 yards, clarity was excellent across the 4x–12x range, with a crisp sight picture and comfortable eye relief.
- **Verdict:** A durable, user-friendly, well-rounded optic for real-world hunting, backed by ZeroTech's no-questions-asked lifetime warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** ZeroTech multi-tool (or coin) — remove the turret centre bolt, re-index to '0'
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Non-illuminated — no battery required
- **Lens covers:** Soft neoprene scope cover (standard Thrive; not HD alloy flip-up)
