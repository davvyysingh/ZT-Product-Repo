# ZeroTech Thrive HD 2.5-15x50 – PHR 2 Illuminated (Adjustable BDC Turret) (THDB2155P-IR)

## Summary
A lightweight 30mm-tube, second-focal-plane 2.5–15×50 HD hunting scope with a 6:1 zoom ratio, illuminated glass-etched PHR 2 reticle and the Adjustable BDC Turret fitted — five shooter-set, lockable distance markers over a full 0.25 MOA scale, with a standard capped turret in the box — equally suited to scrub and to open-country precision, for predator and varmint hunters hitting small targets at distance.

## Key features
- **Illuminated glass-etched PHR 2 reticle calibrated for 15x** — unobtrusive holdover stadia out to 25 MOA on this second-focal-plane design; clean, crisp lines for efficient, deliberate shot placement, with red illumination across six brightness settings to manage low light.
- **Adjustable BDC Turret included and fitted** — five independently settable, lockable distance markers built from your own confirmed drops, with the full 0.25 MOA scale remaining underneath for conventional dialling. Set your drops at the range, dial to the preset marker in the field, and reconfigure on the fly when loads or conditions change.
- **Standard ¼ MOA turret dial and cap included** — for those who prefer a more traditional configuration; turrets run capped or uncapped.
- **6:1 zoom ratio from 2.5x to 15x** — close timber at 2.5x, a basin at 15x; a wide 13.5 m field of view at 100 m finds movement before working it in close.
- **Parallax adjustable from 9.2 m to infinity**, 101mm of eye relief, and a 50mm fully multi-coated objective for first and last light.
- **60 MOA of elevation and windage travel** in 0.25 MOA clicks from a 30mm 6061-T6 maintube; tool-free coin-slot turret reset.
- **Windage turret limiter pin** — fitted to Thrive HD and Thrive X models; limits the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero (removable if preferred — see the manual).
- **Premium, lightweight versatility** — 22.3 oz (631 g) for long carries; IPX7 sealed and fogproof; heavy-duty aluminium flip-up lens covers fitted; backed by the ZeroTech Triple-A Lifetime Warranty, transferable with no paperwork.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk presents the updated Thrive HD 2.5-15x50 as one of the presenter's favourite ZeroTech scopes — a lightweight hunting optic that takes abuse, with clear glass and crisp turrets — shown on a Savage carbon hunter shooting touching groups:
- **Layout:** Fully multi-coated 50mm objective that lets in plenty of light for dawn and dusk shots; 30mm tube; capped windage with the option of capped elevation, open-top elevation or the new BDC turret; side parallax from 10 yards to infinity; push-button illumination on the illuminated model; a super-smooth magnification ring with a position for the removable throw lever; adjustable dioptre.
- **Build and turrets:** 6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium, IPX7 so it can be fully submerged. Second focal plane with PHR 2 or PHR 2 Illuminated reticles; 60 MOA of elevation and windage in ¼ MOA clicks, 15 MOA per revolution. The non-illuminated model weighs just under 22 oz.
- **In the box:** Neoprene scope cover, standard turret and cap, grub screws and Allen key, stickers, lens cloth and reticle chart.
- **Running the turrets:** Capped windage with the BDC turret on top, or refit the standard turret and run it capped or open. Turrets slip with the supplied tool, a brass case or the back of a knife via the keeper slot.
- **Illumination (illuminated model):** Six settings; tap to turn on, hold 3–5 seconds to turn off, and it returns to the last setting.
- **Windage limiter:** What feels like a zero stop on the windage is a small pin that stops the dial going a full revolution, so "L" and "R" can't be confused; 14 MOA of travel, splitting at 7 each side. If you hit it while zeroing, reset the turret to zero and continue; the pin can be removed with a small flat-head screwdriver if you'd rather run without it.
- **Verdict:** A staple backcountry hunting scope for the presenter since his first ZeroTech, now with the redesign and upgraded features.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD / Thrive X manual reference** (`_Thrive_manual.md`) in this folder — follow the **Thrive HD Adjustable BDC Turret (THDB)** sections for the turret reset, BDC procedures and windage limiter. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret reset:** Adjustable BDC elevation turret — unscrew the turret keeper with a small coin, lift the dial, refit at '0' and tighten the keeper with light pressure (no screwdriver); if the BDC dial's index marks sit slightly off the turret body's index, align them via the three 1.5mm hex screws under the top screw; repeat the reset for windage
- **Setting the BDC markers:** Twist the top dial from Locked to the marker number (1–5), rotate that marker to the desired position, then twist back to Locked
- **Switching turrets:** Supplied standard turret dial and cap fit in place of the BDC turret; the thread protector ring must be fitted when the BDC turret is in place
- **Turret index:** 0.25 MOA — approx. 7-8mm at 100 metres (¼" at 100 yards) per click
- **Windage limiter:** Limiter pin restricts the windage dial to 7 MOA either side of zero; if you reach it while zeroing, reset the dial to '0' for more travel, or remove the pin (cannot be refitted)
- **Illumination:** Push-button on the parallax turret — 5 brightness settings + Off, CR2032 battery under the screw-on cover (3-hour auto-off, memory function)
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 9.2m (10yd) to infinity
- **Lens covers:** Heavy-duty aluminium flip-up covers (fitted) plus a neoprene stretch-fit scope cover
