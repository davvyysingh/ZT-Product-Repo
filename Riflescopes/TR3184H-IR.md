## Key features
- **FFP THLR Illuminated reticle** — a first-focal-plane MRAD reticle with a clean layout (central 0.5×0.5 mil box, varied hash marks, and thick outer posts) for intuitive aiming, wind holds, and low-light targeting without clutter; precise holdovers at any magnification.
- **Field-proven robust performance** — refined over two years of real-world testing with the Shoot2Hunt and Rokslide community; engineered to withstand drops, knocks, and backcountry abuse without losing zero.
- **Reliable MRAD turret tracking** — consistent, repeatable adjustments for critical shots.
- **Zero-stop elevation turret** — instant, reliable return to zero after dialling.
- **Capped windage turret** — prevents accidental movement in scrub, packs, and rough terrain.
- **30mm 6061-T6 main tube** — balanced strength-to-weight at 740 g; IPX7 waterproof.
- **Locking dioptre** — maintains reticle focus through extended use and transport.
- **Side parallax (9.2 m to infinity)** and **pre-installed sunshade** — sharp focus across distances with glare control.
- **Illuminated** model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent review tested the Trace Advanced 3-18x44 Shoot2Hunt on a shooting course and across roughly 10 days hunting in Africa:
- **Performance:** Rated a highly capable optic that performs on par with premium options like Nightforce, with the differences in higher-end glass described as minimal.
- **Handling:** A smooth, well-positioned magnification ring that doesn't interfere with fast bolt manipulation, plus tactile, widely spaced turret clicks that are easy to feel and help prevent over-clicking.
- **Zero stop:** A simple, reliable zero-stop system that held zero through airline travel and rough field use.
- **Reticle (highlight):** The clean THLR layout — central 0.5×0.5 mil box, varied hash marks, and thick outer posts — made aiming, wind holds, and low-light targeting intuitive without clutter.
- **Drawback:** The illumination lights only the centre dot rather than the full reticle, which makes wind holds harder in very low light, though it also avoids washing out the target.
- **Verdict:** A well-balanced, durable, highly practical scope offering exceptional usability and value in the $1000–$1500 range.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); windage re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws (no windage zero stop)
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click
- **Illumination:** Push-button digital — 10 brightness settings, CR2032 battery (~2-hour auto-off, resumes previous setting); illuminates the central dot
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 9.2m (10yd) to infinity
- **Sunshade:** Pre-installed (no Tenebraex covers on the 3-18x44)
