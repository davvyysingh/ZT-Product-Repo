# ZeroTech Vengeance LPVO — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for ZeroTech Vengeance low-power variable optics (LPVO): 1-4x24, 1-6x24, 1-8x24, 1-10x28. Individual SKU files link here and note which model-specific sections apply (turret index/click value, reticle, illumination). This manual is separate from the standard Vengeance riflescope manual.

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value/specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Setting the dioptre (do this first)
LPVO dioptre setting differs from standard scopes — it is set at 1× magnification:
1. Loosen the dioptre locking ring and unscrew it away from the ocular several revolutions.
2. Set magnification to 1×.
3. Looking through the scope at an object 15 to 50 metres away, rotate the ocular until the object appears at true 1× magnification (both eyes open, the view through the scope matches the naked eye).
4. Set magnification to full power and confirm the reticle is sharp and crisp edge to edge; make small ocular adjustments if needed.
5. Tighten the locking ring against the ocular, being careful not to rotate the ocular and disturb the setting. Never look through the scope directly at the sun.

## Mounting
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present. ZeroTech recommends professional mounting to preserve the warranty. Fit bases and rings to the manufacturer's torque settings; with ring tops removed, slide the scope to confirm it moves freely (lap rings if needed). Avoid rings contacting the maintube/erector, magnification-ring or objective junctions; confirm no part of the scope touches the barrel or receiver. Tighten ring tops to between 18-22 in lbs. A bubble level is highly recommended for consistent reticle alignment.

## Zeroing
ZeroTech recommends zeroing most centrefire rifles at approximately 100 metres +/- 10 metres (low-magnification scopes may be zeroed closer); rimfire hunting rifles are most commonly zeroed at 50 – 75 metres depending on the ammunition. Bore sight first. Dialling the elevation turret toward the **'U'** symbol moves impact up; the windage turret toward the **'R'** symbol moves it right.

Click values per the manual:
- **0.25 MOA turrets:** approximately 7-8mm at 100 metres (approx. ¼" at 100 yards) per click.
- **0.5 MOA turrets:** approximately 15mm at 100 metres (0.5" at 100 yards), or 7-8mm at 50 metres (0.25" at 50 yards) per click.
- **0.1 MRAD turrets:** 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click.

## Resetting the turrets after zeroing
Once zeroed, remove the windage and elevation turrets using a 2mm hex key. Unscrew and remove the elevation turret centre bolt, slide the turret off and reposition so the '0' index aligns with the vertical index mark on the maintube, then refit and tighten the centre bolt, confirming the '0' position. Repeat for the windage turret.

## Reticle illumination
Vengeance LPVO models have a reticle illumination feature with six brightness settings and an 'Off' position between each setting. Before first use, remove the illumination dial battery cap and discard the plastic circuit blocker, then reinstall the CR2032 button battery and refit the cap. Switch illumination to 'Off' during storage or transport, and carry a spare CR2032 button battery when relying on the illumination feature.

## Battery safety warning
These optics contain a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Scope care
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth.
