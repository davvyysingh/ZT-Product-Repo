## Key features
- **FFP RMG-L Illuminated reticle (LPVO Specific)** — purpose-built for variable-power use: clean and almost red-dot-like at 1× with a bold centre dot and illuminated donut for fast both-eyes-open acquisition, scaling accurately to reveal holdover and windage references out to 10 mils at higher magnification without clutter.
- **True 1× to 10× magnification** — wide situational awareness up close and clear, precise images at 10× for extended shots.
- **ED glass optical system** — superior resolution, light transmission, and colour accuracy with edge-to-edge clarity throughout the zoom range.
- **34mm 6061-T6 aluminium tube** — robust, with significant internal elevation travel (35 MRAD elevation, 15 MRAD windage; around 34 mils usable).
- **Pop-up locking elevation turret with zero stop** plus a **capped windage turret** — dial for distance and return reliably to zero, in 0.1 MRAD clicks at 10 mils per revolution.
- **Industry-leading field of view** — about 117 ft at 100 yards on 1× for near-true-1× speed, a forgiving eyebox, and minimal tunnelling.
- **Tool-less aluminium flip-up lens covers** — rotate and lock at any angle (clearing top-mounted red dots) and a **removable throw lever** for fast magnification changes.
- **Locking dioptre, fixed parallax (100 m), IPX7 waterproof**; illuminated model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech walkthrough (Mac) and two independent reviews present the Trace ED 1-10x24 as a premium LPVO:
- **Optics and build:** A durable 34mm 6061-T6 tube with a 24mm objective and ED glass delivering excellent light transmission and edge-to-edge clarity; impressive 1× performance with a comfortable eyebox, minimal tunnelling, and both-eyes-open shooting, holding strong clarity to 10×.
- **Reticle and illumination:** The new RMG-L FFP reticle looks almost like a red dot at 1× (bold centre dot and illuminated donut) and scales accurately at higher power with holdovers to 10 mils; push-button illumination with five brightness settings, easy cycling, and memory recall of the last-used setting — visible even in daylight.
- **Turrets:** A pop-up/pop-lock zero-stop elevation turret with capped windage, in 0.1 mil increments at 10 mils per revolution and around 34 mils of internal adjustment, letting shooters dial for distance and return reliably to zero.
- **Field of view and handling:** An industry-leading ~117 ft at 100 yards on 1× for fast transitions; tool-less aluminium flip caps that rotate and hold position (clearing top-mounted red dots) and a removable throw lever.
- **Real-world use:** On a .300 Blackout (Wedgetail MPR Mini), it produced tight sub-MOA groups at 100 yards and consistent hits past 200 yards, transitioning seamlessly between close and distant targets across shooting positions — a durable, versatile, premium-feeling LPVO with strong value.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Pop-up locking elevation turret with zero stop (set via 2mm hex grub screws); capped windage turret re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click (10 mils per revolution)
- **Illumination:** Push-button digital — five brightness settings, CR2032 battery (memory recall of last-used setting)
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m — no adjustment
- **Lens covers:** Tool-less aluminium flip-up covers (rotate/position by hand)
