# ZeroTech Thrive / Thrive HD Spotting Scope — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive and Thrive HD 20-60x85 spotting scopes. Individual SKU files link here and note model-specific details (ED glass, variant).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Unconditional lifetime replacement or repair regardless of fault or reason, for the life of the product. Fully transferable — if you sell the spotting scope, the warranty goes with it. (Does not cover intentional loss or damage.)

## Spotting scope features
Key parts/controls: ocular lens, focus adjustment ring, rotation lock thumbscrew, magnification power change ring, rubber-armoured chassis, twist-up eyecup, extendable sunshade, 85mm objective lens, and an ARCA Swiss compatible tripod foot (a standard 1/4×20 camera thread is also included).

## Usage tips
- Never use the spotting scope to look directly at the sun.
- Always mount the scope securely to your tripod head before use. The Thrive series 20-60x85 has an integrated ARCA Swiss dovetail plus a conventional 1/4×20 thread on the base for standard tripod QD plates or direct-thread mounting.
- Make sure your tripod system is rated for a spotting scope — these weigh 1885 g (66.5 oz).
- When acquiring your target, set the magnification power change ring to the lowest setting (20x) for the largest field of view, which makes finding your point of interest easier.
- Use the focus adjustment ring to bring the image into clear focus, then zoom in as needed.

## Caring for your spotting scope
Wipe dust and debris from the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean the lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope (irreversible damage may result). Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech lens cloth.
