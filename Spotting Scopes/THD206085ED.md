## Key features
- **20-60x zoom with a large 85mm Japanese ED objective** — versatile power from wide landscape scanning to fine detail, with abundant light-gathering for bright images even at high magnification.
- **Japanese ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass** — superior colour correction and light transmission, minimising chromatic aberration with edge-to-edge sharpness.
- **Fully broadband multi-coated (FBMC) optics and dielectric-coated prisms** — high contrast, vibrant detail, and strong low-light/shade performance with reduced glare and distortion.
- **Linear zoom mechanism and large focus wheel** — smooth, intuitive, precise control.
- **Argon-filled, IPX6 waterproof chassis** — sealed against dust and heavy rain; rubber-armoured for durability.
- **Built-in ARCA Swiss dovetail plus a 1/4×20 thread** — drops straight onto any ARCA head (no separate plate to forget), or uses standard tripod plates.
- **Angled body with an extendable sunshade and twist-up eyecup**; unconditional lifetime warranty.
