## Key features
- **20-60x zoom with a large 85mm objective** — versatile power and maximum light-gathering for bright, clear images, ideal for range target checking and distant wildlife.
- **Combined prism type with fully broadband multi-coated (FBMC) lenses** — excellent image quality, strong light transmission, and reduced glare.
- **Tube focus system** — smooth, precise focusing for comfortable observation at any distance.
- **Integrated ARCA-compatible tripod adapter plus a 1/4×20 thread** — mounts directly to ARCA heads (no plate needed) or standard tripods.
- **IPX6 waterproof** — reliable performance in varied weather.
- **Comfortable viewing** — 4.1-1.4mm exit pupil and 18-20mm eye relief; adjustable sunshade; angled body.
- **Soft carry case included** — can stay on while the scope is mounted on a tripod; unconditional lifetime warranty.

## Video insights
Two field reviews (including a ZeroTech walkthrough by Mac) rate the Thrive 20-60x85 a feature-rich, great-value backcountry spotter:
- **Optics:** A 20-60x spotter with an 85mm objective for maximum light transmission; the combined prism and fully broadband multi-coated lens give clarity and durability, reduced glare, and strong low-light performance — letting "your eyes do the walking before your feet," to identify a shooter animal before committing miles.
- **Controls:** Ultra-smooth magnification and focus rings, plus an adjustable sun hood to fight glare when you can't keep the sun at your back.
- **Tripod mount (favourite feature):** A built-in ARCA rail means no plate needed — leave a plate on your camera and still run the spotter, avoiding the classic backcountry problem of balancing a plateless scope on a tripod.
- **Build:** Fully waterproof and fog-proof for whatever the weather brings.
- **Package:** Front and rear objective covers, a soft carry case (with a base hole so it can stay on while mounted), a lens cloth, and a warranty card.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive / Thrive HD Spotting Scope manual reference** (`_Thrive_SpottingScope_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Tripod:** Integrated ARCA Swiss dovetail plus a 1/4×20 thread; use a tripod rated for the 1885 g (66.5 oz) weight
- **Usage:** Set the magnification ring to 20x to acquire your target (widest field of view), then use the focus ring; extend the sunshade as needed
- **Care:** Damp microfibre cloth for the body; lens brush and ZT cloth for the lenses; no solvents/sprays; never look at the sun through the scope
