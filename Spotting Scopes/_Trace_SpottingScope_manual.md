# ZeroTech Trace Advanced Spotting Scope — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Trace Advanced 20-60x80 ED spotting scope. The SKU file links here and notes model-specific details (OSR reticle, M-LOK).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable, and covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech will repair or replace the product with one of equal or similar value and/or specifications, and return it to you at no charge.

## Spotting scope features
Rubber-armoured body with an 80mm ED objective lens. M-LOK cover plates conceal an M-LOK interface that fits a 5-slot M-LOK Picatinny rail (and a 7-slot rail) plus various M-LOK accessories. Controls: magnification power change ring, focus adjuster ring, dioptre adjuster, and ocular lens.

## Setting the dioptre
Set the dioptre correctly before first use so the reticle stays sharp through the whole focal range:
1. Set magnification to maximum power.
2. Set the focus to the long (far) focus position.
3. Look (no more than ~5 seconds at a time) at a blank, featureless, bright surface such as a white wall or clear blue sky. **Never look at the sun through the scope.**
4. The reticle should appear sharp immediately; if not, twist the dioptre adjuster until it is sharp. There is about 1¾ turns of adjustment — start with the dioptre assembly twisted flush against the ocular and adjust anticlockwise to the optimal setting.
5. Note the final dioptre position for reference; it shouldn't need frequent adjustment unless someone disturbs it.

## Mounting to a tripod
Multiple options depending on your tripod head:
- **Conventional QD plate:** The base is threaded for conventional tripod QD plates — screw the plate into the base and attach to your head (an anti-rotate index pin hole is provided).
- **Magpul MOE 5-slot Picatinny rail:** Two bolts ahead of the QD position let you fit a Magpul MOE 5-slot rail for a rigid attachment to Picatinny-compatible (often dual ARCA/Picatinny) shooting tripod heads. The fastening bolts and hex key are in the black accessories bag supplied in the box.
- **ARCA Swiss:** ARCA Swiss adapter plates are widely available; the manual notes the GRAYOPS 4-Way Dovetail (Anti-Rotate) ARCA mount as an excellent fit (grayops.com).

## Reticle
The Trace Advanced 20-60x80 ED uses the **OSR (Optimised Spotting Reticle) First Focal Plane** spotting-scope reticle. Refer to the supplied reticle spec sheet / OSR dimensions chart for subtensions.

## Scope care
Wipe dust and debris from the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean the lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the scope (irreversible damage may result). Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech lens cloth.
