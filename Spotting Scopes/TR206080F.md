## Key features
- **OSR (Optimised Spotting Reticle) First-Focal-Plane MRAD reticle** — glass-etched and offset from centre to maximise the field of view; indexed in 0.2 MRAD with 0.1 MRAD measuring bars on the elevation and windage axes for precise shot correction and ranging of known-size targets.
- **20-60x zoom with an 80mm ED objective and FMC coatings** — high resolution, clarity, and strong low-light performance.
- **In-line magnification, focus, and dioptre assembly** — all controls in line for fast, easy manipulation.
- **Generous eye box and oversized eyepiece** — 30mm eye relief reduces eyestrain during long observation.
- **Magpul M-LOK accessory rails at 3, 9, and 12 o'clock** — pop-off covers reveal mounts for a Kestrel wind meter, rangefinder, or red dot; an optional Magpul MOE 5-slot rail at 6 o'clock supports rigid Picatinny tripod mounting.
- **Multiple tripod options** — mounts via conventional QD plate, Picatinny (MOE rail), or ARCA Swiss adapter.
- **Super-broad 10 m-to-infinity focal range; IPX7 waterproof; durable rubber armour**; unconditional lifetime warranty.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace Advanced Spotting Scope manual reference** (`_Trace_SpottingScope_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Dioptre setup (do this first):** Max magnification + far focus, look briefly at a blank bright surface, then twist the dioptre (≈1¾ turns, start flush against the ocular and adjust anticlockwise) until the reticle is sharp; note the position. Never look at the sun through the scope.
- **Mounting:** Conventional QD plate (threaded base), or fit the Magpul MOE 5-slot Picatinny rail using the bolts + hex key in the supplied black accessories bag, or use an ARCA Swiss adapter plate (e.g. GRAYOPS 4-Way Dovetail)
- **Reticle:** OSR First-Focal-Plane — see the supplied OSR dimensions chart for subtensions
- **Care:** Damp microfibre cloth for the body; lens brush and ZT cloth for the lenses; no solvents/sprays
