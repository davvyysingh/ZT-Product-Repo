# ZeroTech Thrive HD Micro Prism — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive HD Micro Prism 1x20 sight. Individual SKU files link here and note any model-specific details (colour, reticle, mounting).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Mounting heights and footprint
The Micro Prism ships with the 1.6" medium-height base fitted and includes four (4) user-configurable base-height options: 1.1" low, 1.4" medium-low, 1.6" medium (fitted), and 1.93" high rise. Each base height has its own marked base-screw set. The sight uses a common Aimpoint Micro / T-2 compatible footprint and can be adapted directly to aftermarket bases and mounts.

## Battery installation
Uses a side-loading CR2032 battery (up to 50,000 hours). Loosen the two (2) T-10 Torx screws and slide the battery-cover retainer assembly away from the sight (do this at a bench so the screws aren't misplaced). Remove the battery and discard the plastic insulator. Install the CR2032 with the positive terminal facing up (writing side up). Refit the cover retainer and the two T-10 Torx screws, tightening to 6 in lbs — do not overtighten.

## Illumination and functions
The reticle is glass-etched and can be used with or without illumination. To activate illumination, press and hold either brightness button for about 3 seconds. Use either brightness button to change settings — there are 10 normal brightness settings plus 3 night-vision (NV) settings (13 total). An auto-sleep/auto-activate function puts the sight to sleep after 3½ minutes undisturbed; it resumes the previously selected brightness as soon as it detects motion. To shut down, hold either brightness button for 3 seconds. ZeroTech recommends shutting the sight down for storage.

## Mounting to the firearm
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present; professional mounting is recommended to preserve the warranty. The sight uses a Picatinny-compatible clamp interface. When swapping bases to set mounting height, apply no more than 14 in lbs to the base screws. To fit to a Picatinny rail, loosen the clamp bolt(s) with a T-10 Torx key for clearance, engage the clamp into a rail slot, apply slight forward (muzzle-ward) pressure, and tighten the clamp bolts to approximately 25–30 in lbs (thread-locking compound is pre-applied; use a non-permanent compound such as Loctite 222 if reapplying). Re-check clamp-bolt torque periodically.

## Windage & elevation adjustments
The windage and elevation index (click) value is 1 MOA — each click shifts impact approximately 1" at 100 yards (½" at 50 yards). Use the included turret key / multi-tool to make adjustments; total windage/elevation adjustment is 90–100 MOA.

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the sight. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth.
