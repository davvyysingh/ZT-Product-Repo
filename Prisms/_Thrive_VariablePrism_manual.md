# ZeroTech Thrive HD Variable Prism (1-4x24) — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive HD Variable Zoom Prism 1-4x24 sight. The individual SKU file links here.

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Features
Key parts/controls: ocular dioptre adjuster, magnification ring, objective, elevation turret, windage turret, illumination brightness controls (+ / –), battery cover, clamp plate, and QD (Quick Detach) lever.

## Battery installation
Uses a CR2032. Use the included polymer coin tool to loosen the battery cover anticlockwise. Remove the battery and discard the plastic circuit blocker located underneath. Reinstall the CR2032 with the positive terminal facing up (writing side up). Refit the cover, carefully engaging the threads, and tighten with the tool until it hits a firm stop.

## Functions
The reticle is glass-etched and can be used with or without illumination. Briefly press either brightness button to activate; use either button to adjust brightness — there are 11 normal brightness settings for each of the green and red modes. Toggle between red and green illumination by pressing and holding both buttons for 3 seconds. Auto-sleep engages after 3½ minutes undisturbed and resumes the previously selected brightness on motion. To shut down, hold either button for 3 seconds (recommended for storage to conserve battery). Note: the product spec sheet lists "10" brightness settings while the printed manual states 11 normal settings per colour — the SKU file reproduces the spec value.

## Mounting
Confirm the firearm is unloaded first; professional mounting is recommended. The sight uses a Picatinny-compatible interface secured by a locking Quick Detach (QD) lever. A second clamp plate is supplied so the QD lever can be reversed (pointing toward the muzzle when locked) to create clearance for controls such as an AR charging handle. To swap the clamp plate: unlock the lever, press it firmly inward at the hinge to expose the nut on the opposite side, remove the nut, remove the clamp plate (taking care not to lose the two springs for the stainless guides), fit the alternate clamp plate, and tighten the nut until adequate grip on the rail is achieved. The sight uses a common Mini ACOG footprint, so it adapts directly to aftermarket bases/mounts (e.g. American Defense AD-B2-C Mini ACOG QD mount).

## Windage & elevation adjustments
The windage/elevation index (click) value is 0.5 MOA — each click shifts impact approximately ½" at 100 yards (¼" at 50 yards). Use the integrated turret key on top of the turret caps.

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe the body with a damp microfibre cloth. Clean lenses with a lens brush and the ZeroTech lens cloth. Never spray cleaners or solvents on any part of the sight. Avoid touching the lenses; remove fingerprints with the ZeroTech cloth. Remove the battery for long-term storage.
