## Key features
- **Glass-etched ZT Prism Dot reticle** — a crisp, consistent aiming point that remains visible without illumination, ideal for daytime use and for shooters with astigmatism.
- **1x magnification with a 20mm objective** — fast, both-eyes-open target acquisition with high situational awareness.
- **13 brightness settings (including 3 night-vision)** — usable across lighting conditions, with a side-loading CR2032 battery rated up to 50,000 hours.
- **Motion-sensor auto sleep / auto on** — conserves battery while keeping the sight ready; resumes the last-used brightness on movement.
- **Four user-configurable mounting heights** — from a low-profile cheek weld up to a 1.93" high rise for night-vision/gear; common Aimpoint Micro / T-2 footprint for aftermarket mounts.
- **CNC-machined 6061-T6 aluminium body** in Flat Dark Earth — shockproof, waterproof, and fog-proof (IPX7).
- **Tenebraex adapter geometry** — accepts 26FCV-010BK1 & 28MMFC-FCV Tactical Tough flip-up lens covers over the multi-coated lenses.
- **1 MOA windage/elevation clicks**; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
An independent review rates the Thrive HD Micro Prism a rugged, feature-rich optic for its price:
- **Eye relief:** Rated around 3.5" but feels even more forgiving in use — you can move well back before noticeable vignetting while keeping a usable sight picture.
- **Reticle:** As an etched-reticle prism, it's especially good for astigmatism — a crisp, distortion-free aiming point like a red dot that stays visible in daylight even if the battery dies.
- **Controls and adjustment:** 13 brightness settings (3 night-vision), with standard MOA adjustments that can be made using common tools like a coin or flathead and around 90 MOA of elevation travel.
- **Mounting:** Four risers give flexibility from a low cheek-weld setup up to 1.93" for night vision or added gear; also compatible with T2-style mounts.
- **Build and package:** Durable 6061-T6 aluminium that's waterproof, fog-proof, and shockproof, with a side-loading battery tray and Tenebraex-style cover compatibility; ships with multiple mounting options and a compact cover — an excellent-value, versatile package.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Micro Prism manual reference** (`_Thrive_MicroPrism_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Picatinny clamp (T-10 Torx, 25-30 in lbs); four base-height options (1.1"/1.4"/1.6"/1.93"), 1.6" fitted; base screws ≤14 in lbs
- **Battery:** Side-loading CR2032 (two T-10 Torx on the cover retainer; positive up; 6 in lbs); up to 50,000 hours
- **Illumination:** 13 settings (10 normal + 3 NV); press/hold a brightness button ~3s to turn on/off; auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake (resumes last setting)
- **Adjustment:** 1 MOA per click (1" at 100yd / ½" at 50yd) via the included turret key
