# ZeroTech Thrive HD Variable Prism 1-4x24 RAP-R Illuminated (THDP1424)

## Summary
A true variable-magnification (1–4×) prism scope — combining prism clarity and battery-free etched-reticle reliability with LPVO-style flexibility — featuring the dual-colour (red/green) illuminated RAP-R reticle, a wide field of view, 0.5 MOA capped turrets, and an integrated Picatinny QD mount on the Mini ACOG footprint.

## Key features
- **True variable 1–4× prism design** — marries the clarity and rugged reliability of a prism optic with the versatility of variable magnification (historically prisms have been fixed-power), for close quarters through mid range.
- **Glass-etched RAP-R reticle with dual-colour illumination** — switch instantly between red and green; the etched reticle stays visible even if the battery dies and is friendly to shooters with astigmatism.
- **Wide field of view** — 131.3 ft at 100 yards on 1× for strong situational awareness and fast, LPVO-style target acquisition.
- **24mm fully multi-coated objective** with ~2.8" eye relief — suits standard ARs as well as platforms where the optic sits further back (e.g. bullpups).
- **0.5 MOA capped windage/elevation turrets** — the caps double as the adjustment tool; ~100 MOA of travel with fixed parallax at 100 m.
- **Integrated Picatinny QD mount with a reversible lever** — orient the lever forward or back to clear controls like an AR charging handle; common Mini ACOG footprint for aftermarket mounts.
- **CR2032 with auto sleep / shake-awake**; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac) introduces the Thrive HD 1–4x Variable Prism as a significant step forward in optic design:
- **Concept:** Presented as the world's first true variable-magnification prism optic, with a continuous 1× to 4× zoom that combines prism clarity with the flexibility usually reserved for LPVOs.
- **Reticle and illumination:** A second-focal-plane RAR/RAP-R reticle with dual-colour illumination — switch instantly between red and green at the press of a button — via large, easy-to-use controls (the reviewer cited up to 13 brightness settings).
- **Adjustment:** Half-MOA clicks with around 100 MOA of elevation travel, using capped windage/elevation turrets whose caps double as the adjustment tool; external battery access for convenience.
- **Handling:** An ultra-smooth magnification ring across the full range, a 24mm objective, around 3" of eye relief (well suited to bullpups and standard ARs), and an impressive ~130 ft field of view at 100 yards on 1× — competitive with high-end LPVOs for fast acquisition.
- **Mounting:** A built-in Picatinny system with an adjustable lever that orients forward or backward to avoid interference with controls like the charging handle — overall an innovative, versatile, compact, capable platform.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Variable Prism manual reference** (`_Thrive_VariablePrism_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Picatinny QD lever; reversible clamp plate (lever toward the muzzle for charging-handle clearance); common Mini ACOG footprint (adapts to aftermarket ACOG QD mounts)
- **Battery:** CR2032 (polymer coin tool; remove the plastic circuit blocker; positive up; tighten to a firm stop)
- **Illumination:** Press either button to activate/adjust; hold both ~3s to toggle red/green; hold either ~3s to shut down; auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake (spec lists 10 settings; the printed manual states 11 per colour)
- **Adjustment:** 0.5 MOA per click (½" at 100yd / ¼" at 50yd) via the integrated turret key
- **Parallax:** Fixed at 100m; set the reticle via the ocular dioptre adjuster
