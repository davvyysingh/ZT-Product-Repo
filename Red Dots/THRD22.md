## Key features
- **Crisp 2 MOA red dot** — fine enough for precise aiming yet fast for snap shots and close-range work.
- **10 digital brightness levels with auto on/off** — tactile push-button control (including night-vision-compatible levels) that retains the last-used setting; shake-awake activates instantly on movement.
- **Fully multi-coated 22mm objective** — a clear sight picture with reduced glare.
- **6061-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium** — shockproof, waterproof, and fog-proof, reliable from -20°C to 70°C.
- **Aimpoint Micro / T2 footprint** — ships with both high and low Picatinny mounts and works with thousands of aftermarket mounting options.
- **0.5 MOA windage/elevation clicks** — precise zeroing, with up to 50,000 hours on a side-loading CR2032.
- Backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac) presents the Thrive HD 1x22 digital red dot as a solid, affordable, versatile optic:
- **Build:** Compact and durable 6061 aircraft-grade aluminium with a 22mm fully multi-coated objective for better light transmission, reduced glare, and durability; shockproof, waterproof, and fog-proof across a wide temperature range.
- **Reticle and controls:** A precise 2 MOA dot with 10 brightness settings (including two night-vision-compatible levels), controlled via top-mounted plus/minus buttons that also power the unit on/off while retaining the last-used brightness.
- **Adjustment:** Elevation and windage in half-MOA increments (the reviewer cited around 90 MOA of total travel).
- **Power:** A side-compartment CR2032 with shake-awake to preserve battery (remember to remove the protective film/tape on install).
- **Mounting and package:** Built on a T2 footprint for wide mount/riser compatibility, shipping with both high and low risers plus mounting/adjustment tools, spare screws, a lens cloth, and warranty materials — delivering fast acquisition and dependable real-world performance.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Digital Red Dot manual reference** (`_Thrive_HD_Digital_RedDot_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Weaver/Picatinny compatible; high and low mounts included; T10 Torx (on the multi-tool) cross bolt with forward pressure, torqued to a minimum of 15 in lbs (threadlocker if needed)
- **Battery:** CR2032 in a side compartment (multi-tool to open; remove the insulator); up to 50,000 hours
- **Illumination:** 10 settings (incl. 2 NV); briefly press + or – to power on/adjust; hold either ~3s to power down (resumes last setting); auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake
- **Adjustment:** 0.5 MOA per click (½" at 100yd)
