## Key features
- **Multi-reticle red dot** — toggle between three patterns (a single dot, a 3 MOA dot inside a 42 MOA circle, or just the 42 MOA circle) for flexibility between fast acquisition and precise aiming at extended pistol distances.
- **1x, 21mm fully multi-coated lens** — a clean, clear sight picture with excellent clarity and durability.
- **Ultra-light 19 g (0.67 oz), ultra-thin profile** — ideal as a compact primary optic for concealed-carry pistols or as a lightweight backup sight.
- **Shield RMSc footprint** — mounts to most compact pistols and RMSc-compatible plates/interfaces.
- **10 adjustable brightness settings** — with the lowest levels suited to night vision.
- **Shake-awake technology (auto sleep / auto on)** — powers down after inactivity and reactivates instantly on movement; up to 50,000 hours on a CR2032 (bottom-mounted for a low deck height).
- **Recoil-proof, waterproof, and fog-proof** construction (IPX7), reliable from -20°C to 70°C.
- **Windage/elevation via 1.5mm hex**; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Micro Reflex (DM21) manual reference** (`_Thrive_DM21_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Shield RMSc footprint; three mounting-screw sets included; fit to the mount/plate and tighten with a T-10 Torx to the mount maker's torque; non-permanent threadlocker recommended
- **Battery:** CR2032 in the base compartment (note – orientation); up to 50,000 hours; remove the optic to replace
- **Illumination:** 10 brightness settings; briefly press + or – to activate, +/- to adjust, hold – for 3s to shut down; auto-sleep after 2½ min with motion wake (resumes last setting)
- **Reticle toggle:** Press and hold + for ~3 seconds to cycle the three reticle patterns
- **Adjustment:** Windage/elevation via a 1.5mm hex key (no defined click value)
