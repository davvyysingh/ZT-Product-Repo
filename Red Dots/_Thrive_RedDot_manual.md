# ZeroTech Thrive Red Dot — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive Red Dot Optic (RDO) — a compact, dial-illuminated micro red dot. Individual SKU files link here and note any model-specific details (reticle, dot size).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Features
Key controls are the elevation turret, windage turret, illumination control dial (rotary), mount interface, and battery cover. The optic uses a tool-free turret system with an integrated turret key, and capped windage/elevation turrets.

## Battery installation
Install a CR2032 battery and remove/discard the plastic insulator. (Brightness is set by the rotary illumination control dial — there is no motion sensor/auto-off, so set the dial to off and remove the battery for long-term storage.)

## Windage & elevation adjustments
Every click on the windage or elevation turret adjusts point of impact by 1 MOA (1" at 100 yards). Use the top of the turret caps (the integrated turret key) to make adjustments — no separate tools needed.

## Mounting
The Thrive RDO is compatible with most Weaver and Picatinny style bases/rails, and the base is interchangeable with popular aftermarket options. Confirm the firearm is unloaded first. Use a T15 Torx driver to loosen the mount interface cross bolt several turns until the interface slips over the rail, ensuring the cross bolt engages a slot. Apply slight forward (muzzle-ward) pressure so the optic seats against the front face of the slot (preventing forward slip under recoil), and tighten the cross bolt to 25 in lbs (approx. 2.8 NM) while maintaining pressure. Re-check the cross bolt remains tight after use.

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe fingerprints from the lenses using only the ZeroTech lens cloth (other materials may damage coatings); remove debris with a lens brush first. Check the mount interface is tight after each shooting session. Avoid solvents and cleaner sprays; if contact occurs, wipe off immediately. Remove the battery for long-term storage.
