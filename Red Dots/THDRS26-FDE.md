## Key features
- **Crisp 3 MOA red dot at 1x** on a 26mm window — a defined aiming point for fast acquisition at close to mid range, with the same sight picture on every host it moves to.
- **10 brightness settings with a locking function** — lock the setting so it holds through a rough day at the range; the bottom two settings are night-vision compatible.
- **Trijicon RMR footprint with both low and high Picatinny mounts included** — drops straight onto existing mounts and slide cuts; one dot usable across pistol slides, rifle mounts and shotgun rails.
- **Shake-awake technology (auto sleep / auto on)** — conserves battery and reactivates on movement; up to 50,000 hours maximum battery life from a top-loading CR2032, replaceable without removing the optic.
- **7075-T6 aluminium chassis in a flat dark earth finish, with an integrated lens shroud** — the shroud takes each knock instead of the glass; shockproof and waterproof (IPX7), rated from -20°C to 70°C.
- **80 MOA of total windage and elevation adjustment** — room to zero on just about any firearm configuration, in 1 MOA clicks.
- **Fully multi-coated optics** — improved coatings reduce glare in bright sun, improve dot brightness in full daylight and add scratch resistance.
- **Four labelled screw sets and a shim included** — direct-mount to popular pistol platforms out of the box; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac) introduces the Thrive HD family of 26mm pistol dots:
- **Build:** 26mm objective in a 7075-T6 aircraft-grade aluminium housing on the RMR footprint. The housing sits proud of the lens, so you can rack on a barricade or with your palm without touching or smudging the glass.
- **Optics:** Improved coatings over the previous Thrive HD dots — less glare in sunny conditions, a brighter dot in very bright ambient light such as full daylight, and better scratch resistance; fully multi-coated and rated from -4°F to 158°F.
- **Power:** Top-mounted battery, so the optic stays on the gun for battery changes; about 50,000 hours on a mid-to-low setting. Shake-awake shuts the dot off after a few minutes without movement and wakes it on the draw — note that constant movement, such as riding in a vehicle, will keep it awake and drain the battery. The reviewer suggests a battery change once a year.
- **Controls:** Plus and minus buttons step through 10 brightness settings, the bottom two night-vision compatible; the dot flashes at the top and bottom of the range. Hold either button for a few seconds to shut down; a tap brings it back at the last setting.
- **Adjustment:** Exposed windage and elevation with about 80 MOA of total travel on each, enough to zero on almost any platform.
- **In the box:** High and low RMR Picatinny mounts for offset on an AR, top-mounting or sub-guns; screw sets in bags labelled by pistol platform; an optional shim for carry-handle or other high mounts that need more downward angle; an Allen key; and a two-sided flat tool that fits both the windage/elevation adjusters and the battery cap.
- **Durability:** The reviewer had run a couple of thousand rounds with periodic zero checks and torture testing, including a host pistol whose front sight post sheared off while the dot kept working. Offered in black and FDE.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Reflex Sight (RS26) manual reference** (`_Thrive_RS26_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Footprint / mounts:** RMR footprint; high riser mount fitted and low mount included; direct-mounts to pre-inlet pistol slides with the four labelled screw sets; aftermarket mounts for offset or piggyback use
- **Battery:** Top-loading CR2032 under the sight-deck cover (included tool or flat-head screwdriver; remove the insulator; positive up)
- **Illumination:** 10 settings; briefly press either button to activate/adjust (dot blinks 3× at min/max); press + and – together to lock or unlock (1 blink locked, 2 blinks unlocked); hold either button 3s to shut down; auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake
- **Picatinny fitment:** T-10 Torx clamp bolt(s) to approx. 15–20 in lbs (Loctite 222 if desired)
- **Adjustment:** 1 MOA per click (1" at 100yd / ½" at 50yd) via the included multi tool or a flat-head screwdriver
