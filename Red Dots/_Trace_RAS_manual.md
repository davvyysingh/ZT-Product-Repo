# ZeroTech Trace R.A.S Red Dot — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Trace R.A.S (Rapid Aiming System) 1x25 Digital Red Dot. The individual SKU file links here.

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Features
Key parts/controls: elevation turret, windage turret, illumination control buttons (+ / –), battery cover, and the rail mount interface — supplied with both a low interface-height rail mount (included) and a high rail mount interface.

## Battery installation
Uses a CR2032. Use the included multi-tool (plastic battery-cover tool) to remove the battery cover, remove the battery and discard the plastic insulator, then reinstall the battery and tighten the cover with the multi-tool until it sits flush with the sight body.

## Button functions
Power on by briefly pressing the + or – button; adjust brightness with the + or – buttons. If left without movement for approximately 3½ minutes the sight enters sleep mode and automatically switches on again on the slightest movement. Press and hold either + or – for about 3 seconds to power down (briefly press either button to power back on — it resumes the previous brightness setting). Power the sight down for short-term storage.

## Windage & elevation adjustments
The printed manual states each click adjusts point of impact by 0.5 MOA (≈ ½" at 100 yards). Note: the TRRD125 spec sheet lists the turret index value as 1 MOA — the SKU file reproduces the spec value; confirm against your unit.

## Mounting
Compatible with most Weaver and Picatinny style bases/rails; ships with high and low Picatinny mounts (Aimpoint Micro / T-2 footprint). Confirm the firearm is unloaded first. Use the included T10 Torx driver (on the multi-tool) to loosen the mount interface cross bolt until the interface slips over the rail, ensuring the cross bolt engages a slot. Apply slight forward (muzzle-ward) pressure and, maintaining it, tighten the cross bolt to 20 in lbs (approx. 2.3 Nm). Re-check it stays tight after use; apply non-permanent thread-locking compound if necessary.

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe fingerprints from the lenses using only the ZeroTech lens cloth (other materials may damage coatings); remove debris with a lens brush first. Check the mount interface is tight after each shooting session. Avoid solvents and cleaner sprays; if contact occurs, wipe off immediately. Remove the battery for long-term storage and store it responsibly, out of reach of children.
