## Key features
- **Enclosed Heavy-duty Aspherical Lens Optic (H.A.L.O)** — the enclosed emitter keeps out water, debris, and gas blow-back, while the aspherical (convex) fully multi-coated lens gives an exceptionally wide horizontal field of view with minimal distortion and strong glare resistance.
- **Crisp 3 MOA red dot with 10 brightness settings** (including night-vision levels) — sharp and bright across daylight, low light, and NV.
- **Top-loading CR2032 battery** — replace without removing the optic or losing zero; up to 50,000 hours, with auto-sleep and shake-awake.
- **High-strength 7075 aluminium housing in Flat Dark Earth** — shockproof, waterproof, and fog-proof, with a frame lip extending beyond the glass for added protection when racking the slide on a barrier.
- **Oversized side buttons with a button-lock function** — press both to lock/unlock, preventing accidental brightness changes during concealed carry.
- **Shield RMSc footprint with an included Picatinny rail adapter** — direct-mounts to many compact pistols, or runs on subguns, ARs, and offset setups.
- **1.5 MOA clicks (80 MOA travel)** with tactile, accurate tracking; an included 1° base shim aids zeroing on taller setups; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech walkthrough plus an independent review (tested on a SIG P322 for high-volume use) rate the Trace H.A.L.O a standout enclosed red dot:
- **Optics:** The aspherical, convex multi-coated lens delivers a wide, clear field of view — more horizontal field of view than almost any other pistol red dot — with minimal distortion, excellent brightness, and strong glare resistance; the 3 MOA dot is sharp and precise across daylight, low light, and night vision.
- **Build:** A robust 7075 aluminium enclosed housing with a frame lip extending beyond the glass (clearance for racking the slide on a barrier) and excellent sealing against water, debris, and gas blow-back — ideal for suppressed use; it also has a rear sight notch for backup aiming if the battery fails.
- **Battery and controls:** A top-loading battery (oversized slot — usable with a coin or brass) changes without removing the optic or losing zero, with up to 50,000 hours and shake-awake; two oversized side buttons give 10 brightness settings (two night-vision) and a lock function (hold both) for concealed carry.
- **Mounting and use:** RMSc footprint with an included low Picatinny rail adapter for subguns, ARs, or offset; 80 MOA of travel with tactile, accurate 1.5 MOA clicks; reviewers favoured it for competition (fast target transitions), training, and defensive use.
- **Package and shim:** Ships with the Picatinny mount, protective cover, tools, screws, lens cloth, and an important 1° shim — because the H.A.L.O sits tall, some pistols run out of zeroing travel at 25 yards, and the shim provides the slight tilt needed to zero correctly.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace H.A.L.O Enclosed Reflex manual reference** (`_Trace_HALO_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Footprint / mount:** Shield RMSc; supplied Picatinny base (two countersunk T-10 bolts ~15 in lbs; clamp bolt ~15–20 in lbs); direct pistol mount with the correct countersunk bolts only
- **Base shim:** Install the included 1° polymer shim if zeroing needs more than 30 MOA of elevation (thickest end toward the muzzle for large "up", toward the shooter for large "down")
- **Battery:** Top-loading CR2032 (plastic cover tool); swap without losing zero; up to 50,000 hours
- **Illumination:** 10 settings; press either button to activate/adjust; lock/unlock by pressing both; hold either 3s to shut down; auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake
- **Adjustment:** 1.5 MOA per click (1.5" at 100yd / ¾" at 50yd) via the included flat-head driver
