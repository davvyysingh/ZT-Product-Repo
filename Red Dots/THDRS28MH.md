# ZeroTech Thrive HD Reflex Sight Multi Reticle High Mount (THDRS28MH)

## Summary
A full-size 28mm open reflex sight on the RMR footprint with a switchable multi-reticle (dot, dot-and-circle, or circle), 11 brightness settings, shake-awake technology, up to 50,000 hours of battery life, and a high Picatinny mount — versatile for rifles, AR platforms, pistols, and shotgun patterning.

## Key features
- **Switchable multi-reticle (red)** — toggle between a single 3 MOA dot, a dot inside a 40 MOA circle, or just the 40 MOA circle, for fast acquisition or precise aiming (the circle suits shotgun patterning).
- **Large 28mm window with 11 brightness settings** — adapt the reticle to changing light.
- **RMR footprint with a high Picatinny mount** — broad compatibility; also direct-mounts to pre-inlet pistol slides or accepts aftermarket mounts (piggyback, 45° offset, etc.).
- **Shake-awake technology (auto sleep / auto on)** — conserves battery and reactivates instantly on movement; up to 50,000 hours on a side-loading CR2032 (replaceable without removing the optic).
- **7075 aluminium construction** — waterproof and shockproof (IPX7), with a protective lens hood extending slightly beyond the glass; reliable from -20°C to 70°C.
- **AR / multi-coated lens** — improves clarity, reduces glare, and gives a true-to-life, tint-free image.
- **1 MOA windage/elevation clicks** (T10 Torx); backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Reflex Sight (RS28) manual reference** (`_Thrive_RS28_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Footprint / mount:** RMR footprint; supplied with a High Picatinny mount; also direct-mount (pistols) or aftermarket (offset/piggyback)
- **Battery:** Side-loading CR2032 (two T6 Torx on the cover retainer; positive up; 6 in lbs)
- **Illumination:** 11 settings; briefly press either button to activate/adjust; hold the + button for 2s to shut down; auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake
- **Reticle toggle:** Press and hold the – button for ~2 seconds to cycle the three reticle patterns
- **Picatinny fitment:** T15 Torx clamp bolt(s) to approx. 30 in lbs (Loctite 222 if desired)
- **Adjustment:** 1 MOA per click (1" at 100yd / ½" at 50yd) via a T10 Torx key
