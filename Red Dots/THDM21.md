## Key features
- **Sharp 3 MOA red dot reticle** on a 1x, 21mm fully multi-coated lens — a clean, clear sight picture for fast target acquisition.
- **Ultra-light 19 g (0.67 oz), ultra-thin profile** — ideal as a compact primary optic for concealed-carry pistols or as a lightweight backup sight.
- **Shield RMSc footprint** — mounts to most compact pistols and RMSc-compatible plates/interfaces.
- **10 adjustable brightness settings** — with the lowest levels suited to night vision.
- **Shake-awake technology (auto sleep / auto on)** — powers down after inactivity and reactivates instantly on movement; up to 150,000 hours on a CR2032 (bottom-mounted for a low deck height).
- **Recoil-proof, waterproof, and fog-proof** construction (IPX7), reliable from -20°C to 70°C.
- **AR / multi-coated lens** developed for minimal image distortion.
- **Windage/elevation via 1.5mm hex**; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Two independent reviews assess the Thrive HD Micro Reflex as a top concealed-carry option:
- **Platform fit:** A micro, ultra-thin optic designed for concealed-carry pistols like the Glock 43X/48, SIG P365, Springfield Hellcat, and Shadow Systems CR920; on a 43X it sits low enough to retain a lower-third iron-sight co-witness.
- **Reticle line-up:** Offered as a 3 MOA red dot, a 3 MOA green dot, or a multi-reticle version that switches between a single dot, a 3 MOA dot inside a 42 MOA circle, or just the 42 MOA circle.
- **Optics:** The 21mm fully multi-coated (slightly squared) glass draws consistent praise for clarity and a clean sight picture, in a sleek blacked-out body with minimal branding.
- **Controls and power:** 10 brightness settings (lowest are night-vision compatible) with dedicated up/down buttons and hold functions for power-off and reticle switching; shake-awake conserves battery (reviewers cited up to ~50,000 hours of use).
- **Practicalities:** A bottom-mounted battery requires removing the optic to swap it — the trade-off for a slimmer, more concealable profile; windage/elevation use a 1.5mm hex key. Ships with a rubber cover, lens cloth, adjustment tool, and multiple screw sets — a compact, durable, versatile package.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Micro Reflex (DM21) manual reference** (`_Thrive_DM21_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Shield RMSc footprint; three mounting-screw sets included; fit to the mount/plate and tighten with a T-10 Torx to the mount maker's torque; non-permanent threadlocker recommended
- **Battery:** CR2032 in the base compartment (note – orientation); up to 150,000 hours; remove the optic to replace
- **Illumination:** 10 brightness settings; briefly press + to activate, +/- to adjust, hold – for 3s to shut down; auto-sleep after 2½ min with motion wake (resumes last setting)
- **Adjustment:** Windage/elevation via a 1.5mm hex key (no defined click value)
