# ZeroTech Thrive HD Micro Reflex (DM21) — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive HD Micro Reflex sights on the Shield RMSc footprint (THDM21 red dot, THDM21G green dot, THDMM21 multi-reticle). Individual SKU files link here and note any model-specific details (reticle colour, multi-reticle toggle).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Footprint and build
Built on the Shield RMSc footprint for compatibility with most compact pistols and RMSc-compatible mounting interfaces; fitted with a moulded rubber protective cover. Recoil-proof, waterproof, and fog-proof.

## Battery installation and sight fitment
Three (3) sets of mounting screws are included — choose the correct set for your application. Install the CR2032 battery into the battery compartment on the base of the sight, observing the orientation marked by the – symbol. Fit the sight to the mount or mounting plate (taking care not to dislodge the battery), then insert the correct screws and tighten with a T-10 Torx driver to the torque specified by the mount/plate manufacturer. Non-permanent thread-locking compound is recommended.

## Functions — THDM21 & THDM21G
Briefly press the + button to activate. Use either brightness button (+ or –) to adjust across the 10 brightness settings. An auto-sleep/auto-activate function puts the sight to sleep after 2½ minutes undisturbed and resumes the previously selected brightness on motion. To shut down, press and hold the – button for 3 seconds (reactivate with a brief press of +). Shut the sight down for storage.

## Functions — THDMM21 (multi-reticle only)
Briefly press + or – to activate. Use + or – to adjust across the 10 brightness settings. To toggle between the three (3) reticle options, press and hold the + button for about 3 seconds until the reticle pattern changes. Auto-sleep after 2½ minutes undisturbed, resuming on motion; press and hold – for 3 seconds to shut down (reactivate with + or –).

## Windage & elevation adjustments
Use a 1.5mm hex key to turn the windage and elevation hex screws; turning the screws shifts impact in the direction indicated on the sight. (No defined click/index value.)

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe fingerprints from the lenses using only the ZeroTech lens cloth (other materials may damage the coatings); remove debris with a lens brush first. Check the mount interface is tight after each shooting session. Avoid solvents and cleaner sprays; if contact occurs, wipe off immediately. Remove the battery for long-term storage and store it responsibly, out of reach of children.
