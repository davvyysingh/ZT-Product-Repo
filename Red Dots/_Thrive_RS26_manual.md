# ZeroTech Thrive HD Reflex Sight (RS26) — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive HD Reflex Sight RS26 (26mm, RMR footprint). Individual SKU files link here and note model-specific details (finish). The Black and FDE versions share the same body, mounts and controls.

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge. (Does not cover intentional loss or damage.)

## Footprint, mounts, and build
Uses the Trijicon RMR footprint. Mounting options are a high rise mount, a low mount, or direct mount (RMR). The sight ships with both Picatinny mounts and a moulded rubber protective cover. Controls and adjustment points: windage and elevation adjustments (1 MOA per click), illumination brightness control buttons (10 settings), top battery cover, T-10 drive mounting bolts and the rail clamp bolt (15–20 in lbs).

## Battery installation
Top-loading CR2032. Loosen the battery cover on the top of the sight deck using the tool provided or an appropriately sized flat-head screwdriver. Remove the battery and discard the plastic insulator. Install the CR2032 directly into the sight with the positive terminal facing up. Refit the battery cover, taking care to ensure proper thread engagement before tightening, and check the sight is functioning.

## Functions
Briefly press either illumination brightness control button to activate the sight. Use either button to adjust across the 10 brightness settings; the dot blinks three times when the minimum or maximum setting is reached.

**Lock function:** briefly press both buttons (+ and –) together to lock the brightness setting — the dot blinks once to confirm lock mode. Briefly press both buttons together again to unlock — the dot blinks twice. While locked, the side buttons will not change the brightness.

**Auto sleep / auto activate:** if the sight is left undisturbed for 3½ minutes it enters sleep mode; as soon as it detects motion it resumes the previously selected brightness. To shut down, hold either brightness button for three seconds (reactivate with a brief press). Shut the sight down for storage.

## Fitting to your firearm
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present; professional mounting is recommended. Three approaches:
1. **Picatinny rail** — the sight comes with high and low mounts for direct Picatinny fitment (typically rifles; some shotguns and revolvers with a rail).
2. **Direct mount** — pistols with pre-inlet slides accept the sight directly; remove the Picatinny mount and set it aside. Four sets of direct-mount screws to suit various pistols are included, each set individually packaged and labelled.
3. **Aftermarket mounts** — enable options such as piggyback on a riflescope, a 45° offset on an AR, or mounting to a spotting scope; ask your local store.

## Picatinny rail fitment
Use a T-10 Torx key to loosen the clamp bolt(s) slightly for clearance over the rail, set the desired position, apply slight forward (muzzle-ward) pressure, and tighten the T-10 clamp bolt(s) to approximately 15–20 in lbs with a torque wrench. Re-check periodically; use a non-permanent thread-locking compound such as Loctite 222 if desired.

## Windage & elevation adjustments
The windage and elevation index (click) value is 1 MOA — each click shifts impact approximately 1" at 100 yards (½" at 50 yards). Use the included multi tool or a flat-head screwdriver to make adjustments.

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 3V coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. Install correctly per polarity; do not mix old/new or different battery types; do not recharge, force-discharge, disassemble, heat or incinerate. Always secure the battery compartment; if it will not close securely, stop using the product and remove the battery. Recycle or dispose of used batteries according to local regulations, never in household rubbish. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) or your local poison control centre immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe fingerprints from the lenses using only the ZeroTech lens cloth (other materials may damage coatings); remove debris with a lens brush first. Check the mount interface is tight after each shooting session. Avoid solvents and cleaner sprays; if contact occurs, wipe off immediately. Remove the battery for long-term storage and store it out of reach of children.
