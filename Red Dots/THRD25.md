## Key features
- **Crisp 3 MOA red dot** on a 1x, 20mm fully multi-coated lens — a balance of speed and precision with a wide field of view for strong situational awareness.
- **11 brightness settings via a rotary illumination control dial** — fully daytime-visible levels for low light through bright sun.
- **Tool-free capped windage/elevation turrets with an integrated turret key** — 1 MOA clicks, no separate tools needed to zero.
- **Weaver/Picatinny-compatible interchangeable base** — fits a wide variety of aftermarket and quick-detach mounting options on the universal Aimpoint Micro footprint.
- **Shockproof, waterproof, and fog-proof** construction (IPX7) built to withstand heavy recoil; reliable from -20°C to 70°C.
- **AR / multi-coated lens** — clear viewing with reduced glare.
- **CR2032 battery** (up to 5,000 hours); backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive Red Dot manual reference** (`_Thrive_RedDot_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Weaver/Picatinny-compatible interface; T15 Torx cross bolt with forward (muzzle-ward) pressure, torqued to 25 in lbs; interchangeable base
- **Battery:** CR2032 (remove the plastic insulator); no auto-off — set the dial to off and remove the battery for long-term storage
- **Illumination:** 11 settings via the rotary illumination control dial
- **Adjustment:** 1 MOA per click (1" at 100yd) via the integrated turret key (capped turrets)
