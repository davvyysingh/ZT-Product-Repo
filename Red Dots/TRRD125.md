## Key features
- **True 1x with a 25mm fully multi-coated objective** — a wide, unobstructed field of view (aided by a recessed LED diode) with reduced glare and improved clarity for strong situational awareness.
- **Sharp 2 MOA red dot** — balances fast target acquisition with precision at extended distances.
- **10 digital brightness settings with shake-awake** — easy +/- push-button control that conserves battery and stays instantly ready.
- **Up to 50,000 hours on a CR2032** — a side-loading, oversized battery slot accessible with a coin or flathead (annual replacement recommended).
- **High and low Picatinny mounts included** — plus compatibility with most Aimpoint Micro / T-2 bases and mounts.
- **Premium Trace-line build** — upgraded glass, improved machining, and a durable, fully enclosed housing that's fogproof, shockproof, and waterproof.
- Ships with a multi-tool; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
A ZeroTech Tech Talk (Mac) presents the Trace R.A.S as a premium AR red dot:

- **Optics and reticle:** A true 1× sight with a 25mm fully multi-coated objective that reduces glare and improves clarity, paired with a 2 MOA dot for fast acquisition and precision at distance.
- **Controls and power:** Top-mounted brightness buttons with 10 settings; a side-loading battery compartment with an oversized slot (coin/flathead) delivering up to 50,000 hours (annual replacement recommended); windage/elevation described in half-MOA increments.
- **Package:** Includes a rubber lens cover, lens cloth, a low mount with an additional height option, and a multi-tool (Torx and flathead) for mounting, battery changes, and adjustments — a durable, high-quality, practical choice for AR platforms and general shooting.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace R.A.S Red Dot manual reference** (`_Trace_RAS_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Mounting:** Weaver/Picatinny compatible; high and low mounts included; T10 Torx (on the multi-tool) cross bolt with forward pressure, torqued to 20 in lbs (threadlocker if needed)
- **Battery:** CR2032, side-loading (multi-tool to open; remove the insulator); up to 50,000 hours
- **Illumination:** 10 settings; briefly press + or – to power on/adjust; hold either ~3s to power down (resumes last setting); auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake
- **Adjustment:** 1 MOA per click per the spec sheet (note: the printed manual and the video state 0.5 MOA — confirm against your unit)
