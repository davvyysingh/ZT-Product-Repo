# ZeroTech Thrive HD Reflex Sight (RS28) — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Thrive HD Reflex Sight (28mm, RMR footprint) family. Individual SKU files link here and note model-specific details (reticle colour/type, mount height). Variants share the same body and controls; the suffix encodes reticle (red / G = green / M = multi-reticle) and mount height (H = high, L = low).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Footprint, mounts, and build
Uses the Trijicon RMR footprint and ships with a Picatinny-compatible base in either a Low (THDRS28L) or High (THDRS28H) version; fitted with a moulded rubber protective cover. Construction is 6061-T6 aluminium, waterproof and shockproof, with AR-coated optics.

## Battery installation
Side-loading CR2032. Loosen the two (2) T6 Torx screws and slide the battery-cover retainer assembly away from the sight. Remove the battery and discard the plastic insulator. Install the CR2032 with the positive terminal facing up. Refit the cover retainer (a small amount of pressure is needed to clip the cradle over the battery) and the two T6 Torx screws, tightening to 6 in lbs — do not overtighten.

## Functions — THDRS28 & THDRS28G (single reticle)
Briefly press either brightness button to activate. Use either button to adjust across the 11 brightness settings. Auto-sleep engages after 3½ minutes undisturbed and resumes the previously selected brightness on motion. To shut down, hold either brightness button for 2 seconds (reactivate with a brief press). Shut the sight down for storage.

## Functions — THDRS28M & THDRS28ML (multi-reticle only)
Briefly press either brightness button to activate; either button adjusts across the 11 brightness settings. To toggle between the three (3) reticle options, press and hold the – button for about 2 seconds until the reticle pattern changes. Auto-sleep after ~3½ minutes undisturbed, resuming on motion. To shut down, press and hold the + button for 2 seconds (reactivate with a brief press).

## Fitting to your firearm
Confirm the firearm is unloaded with no ammunition present; professional mounting is recommended. Three approaches:
1. **Picatinny rail** — the sight comes fitted with a low or high mount for direct Picatinny fitment (typically rifles; some shotguns/revolvers with a rail).
2. **Direct mount** — some pistols with pre-inlet slides accept the sight directly (the Picatinny mount can be removed and set aside).
3. **Aftermarket mounts** — enable options such as piggyback on a riflescope, a 45° offset on an AR, or mounting to a spotting scope.

## Picatinny rail fitment
Use a T15 Torx key to loosen the clamp bolt(s) for clearance over the rail, set the desired position, apply slight forward (muzzle-ward) pressure, and tighten the T15 clamp bolt(s) to approximately 30 in lbs. Re-check periodically; use a non-permanent thread-locking compound such as Loctite 222 if desired.

## Windage & elevation adjustments
The windage and elevation index (click) value is 1 MOA — each click shifts impact approximately 1" at 100 yards (½" at 50 yards). Use a T10 Torx key to make adjustments.

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 3V coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. Install correctly per polarity; do not mix old/new or different battery types; do not recharge, heat, or incinerate. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe fingerprints from the lenses using only the ZeroTech lens cloth (other materials may damage coatings); remove debris with a lens brush first. Check the mount interface is tight after each shooting session. Avoid solvents and cleaner sprays; if contact occurs, wipe off immediately. Remove the battery for long-term storage.
