# ZeroTech Trace H.A.L.O Enclosed Reflex Sight — Manual Reference

Shared setup, care, warranty, and safety reference for the ZeroTech Trace H.A.L.O (Heavy-duty Aspherical Lens Optic) enclosed reflex sights on the Shield RMSc footprint. Individual SKU files link here and note model-specific details (reticle colour, body colour).

## Lifetime "Triple A" warranty
*Any Owner, Any Problem, Always Covered.* Fully transferable; covers accidental damage as well as defects in materials and workmanship. No receipt or warranty card required. ZeroTech repairs or replaces with equal or similar value and/or specifications and returns the product to you at no charge.

## Features
Key parts/controls: battery cover (top), illumination brightness controls (+ / –), elevation and windage adjustments, mount bolts, and rail clamp bolts. The enclosed-emitter design protects the optic in harsh conditions.

## Battery installation
Top-loading CR2032 — unscrew the battery cover on top of the sight using the plastic battery-cover tool, remove the battery and discard the plastic insulator, then reinstall and tighten the cover until flush. The top-loading design lets you change the battery without losing zero.

## Base shim installation (IMPORTANT)
A polymer base shim (a shallow wedge) is included to angle the sight up or down relative to the bore. Zero the firearm first without the shim. If more than 30 MOA of elevation adjustment (up or down) is needed to zero, install the shim to recentre the dot within the sight's mechanical elevation travel (reducing parallax error), then re-zero. Install with the thickest end toward the muzzle if you needed large "up" adjustment, or toward the shooter if you needed large "down" adjustment. (Reference: 45 MOA = 30 clicks at 1.5 MOA/click.)

## Fitting the Picatinny rail base
A Picatinny-compatible base is supplied for most 1913 Picatinny/Weaver rails. Attach the base to the sight using the two (2) countersunk-head T-10 bolts provided (thread-locker is pre-applied; torque ~15 in lbs — use only the correct countersunk bolts). Fit the sight to the rail and tighten the T-10 rail clamp bolt to ~15–20 in lbs. Check bolts periodically. The sight also uses the Shield RMSc micro footprint for direct mounting to many narrow-slide compact pistols (use only the correct countersunk bolts — incorrect bolts can damage the sight and void warranty).

## Functions
Briefly press either brightness button to activate; use either button to adjust across the 10 brightness settings. Auto-sleep engages after 3½ minutes undisturbed and resumes the previously selected brightness on motion. A button-lock function prevents accidental presses — briefly press both + and – together (the dot blinks once to confirm lock; sleep is disabled while locked); press both again to unlock (the dot blinks twice). To shut down, hold either button for 3 seconds (briefly press to reactivate). Shut the sight down for storage.

## Windage & elevation adjustments
The windage/elevation index (click) value is 1.5 MOA — each click shifts impact approximately 1.5" at 100 yards (¾" at 50 yards). Use the included flat-head driver (or an appropriately sized flat-head screwdriver).

## Battery safety warning
Contains a CR2032 coin battery. Keep coin batteries (new or used) out of reach of children — a swallowed or inserted battery can cause severe or fatal injuries in 2 hours or less. If ingestion is suspected, call the 24-hour Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 (Australia) immediately.

## Sight care
Wipe fingerprints from the lenses using only the ZeroTech lens cloth (other materials may damage coatings); remove debris with a lens brush first. Check the mount interface is tight after each shooting session. Avoid solvents and cleaner sprays; if contact occurs, wipe off immediately. Remove the battery for long-term storage.
