## Key features
- **Green multi-reticle system** — switch between a 3 MOA dot, a 40 MOA circle, or a dot-and-circle combination for fast acquisition or precise aiming.
- **Green-dot advantage** — sits closer to the eye's peak sensitivity for sharper daylight visibility and clearer focus, with reduced distortion and eye strain for shooters with astigmatism.
- **Flat Dark Earth finish** — complements modern pistol and rifle platforms.
- **Large 28mm window with 11 brightness settings** — adapt the reticle to changing light.
- **RMR footprint with a low (dual-bolt) Picatinny base** — a lower sight line for co-witness/offset; also direct-mounts to pre-inlet pistol slides or accepts aftermarket mounts.
- **Shake-awake technology (auto sleep / auto on)** — conserves battery and reactivates instantly on movement; up to 50,000 hours on a side-loading CR2032 (replaceable without removing the optic — no re-zero needed).
- **Shockproof, waterproof, and fog-proof** (IPX7); lightweight at 73 g.
- **1 MOA windage/elevation clicks**; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Thrive HD Reflex Sight (RS28) manual reference** (`_Thrive_RS28_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Footprint / mount:** RMR footprint; supplied with a Low (dual-bolt) Picatinny base; also direct-mount (pistols) or aftermarket (offset/piggyback)
- **Battery:** Side-loading CR2032 (two T6 Torx on the cover retainer; positive up; 6 in lbs)
- **Illumination:** 11 settings; briefly press either button to activate/adjust; hold the + button for 2s to shut down; auto-sleep after 3½ min with motion wake
- **Reticle toggle:** Press and hold the – button for ~2 seconds to cycle the three reticle patterns
- **Picatinny fitment:** T15 Torx clamp bolt(s) to approx. 30 in lbs (Loctite 222 if desired)
- **Adjustment:** 1 MOA per click (1" at 100yd / ½" at 50yd) via a T10 Torx key
