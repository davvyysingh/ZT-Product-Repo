# ZeroTech Reticle Reference (Consolidated)

A single reference consolidating every reticle manual and range chart held in this folder. It covers each reticle's measurement units, focal plane / magnification notes, illumination, and the labelled dimension callouts from each manual (reproduced verbatim). Dimension letters (A, B, C… and numbered thickness callouts) correspond to the labelled diagram in each source PDF; refer to the PDF for the matching illustration. To find which product uses a given reticle, see the `Reticle type` field in the product specs.

## How to read these specs

- **Units.** Reticles are calibrated in either **MOA** (minute of angle) or **MRAD/MIL** (milliradian). The unit is stated per reticle and must not be mixed.
- **Focal plane.** In a **First Focal Plane (FFP)** reticle the subtensions are true at every magnification. In a **Second Focal Plane (SFP)** reticle the stated subtensions are only true at one calibrated magnification — SFP manuals/charts carry the note *"refer to correct operating magnification power."*
- **Illumination.** Where a reticle has an illuminated version, the etched geometry is identical to the non-illuminated version; only the illuminated aiming elements differ. Illuminated and non-illuminated manuals in this folder are otherwise the same document.
- **"thick" / "tall" / "dia".** Where a callout is annotated `thick`, `tall`, or `dia`, the value describes line thickness, element height, or dot diameter respectively (rather than a spacing).

## Range charts (shared worksheet)

Every reticle range chart in this folder is a **blank fill-in worksheet**, not a table of pre-computed values. Each chart contains the reticle diagram plus:

- **Rifle and Cartridge Details:** Cartridge · Projectile · BC (Ballistic Coefficient) · Velocity · H.O.B. (Height Over Bore) · Twist Rate · Zero Distance.
- **Elevation worksheet:** a distance column with blank elevation fields for the shooter to complete for their own load — rows at **100, 150, 200, 225, 250, 275, 300, 325, 350, 375, 400, 425, 450, 475, 500, 525, 550, 575, 600 m**.
- SFP reticles repeat the note *"refer to correct operating magnification power."*

The PHR 2 / PHR 3 charts add a holdover-distance annotation (write the range next to the elevation stadium that matches your bullet drop).

### TREMOR3 (Horus TREMOR3) — MIL, FFP, illuminated
A Horus Vision grid reticle; the field guide is descriptive rather than a simple dimension table. Key features:

- **Horus Grid** — an expanded, uncluttered milling grid below the primary horizontal stadia for holds and corrections.
- **Rapid Range Bars** — ranging bars above the primary horizontal stadia; **Tremor ranging chevrons** provide vertical and horizontal target ranging. Refined milling chevrons are calibrated in mil-radians (0.1 mil chevron; 0.1 spacing).
- **Moving Target Holds** — mover reference points calibrated in even mph increments from 2 to 10 mph, closely approximating the 7.62×51mm (.308) profile out to 400 m. The horizontal stadia also carries standard mil-radian graduations for traditional lead holds.

  | MPH | Lead (mils) |
  | --- | --- |
  | 2 | 1.25 |
  | 4 | 2.5 |
  | 6 | 3.75 |
  | 8 | 5 |
  | 10 | 6.5 |

- **Time of Flight (ToF) Wind Dots** — seven patented wind dots (the 4th enlarged for rapid identification), 0.2 mil-radian graduations for standard hold-offs, enabling fast wind holds. The guide details determining the 2nd-wind-dot value for a specific ballistic profile (calibrate on the 4th mil line / 2nd wind dot ≈ 0.95 mil sub-tension; halve the value for ToF dots).
- **Accuracy 1st Speed Shooting Formula** — the 0.1-mil "staircase" pattern above the primary horizontal stadia (0.5 mil at the outside up to 1 mil at the primary vertical stadia), used with the "rule of 10" to establish a quick elevation hold for a known-size target (e.g. 12″ target). Worked example drop table (7.62×51 "10 gun"): 381 m → 2 mil, 435 m → 3 mil, 508 m → 4 mil, 610 m → 5 mil.
- **Dialing elevation** — hold, dial, or dial-and-hold; recommended to hold out to 10 mil of elevation and use the calibrated wind dots, dialing beyond 10 mil and holding wind on the 0.2 mil graduations.
- **Illumination** — illuminated aiming points at the centre, on the primary vertical stadia every 2 mil below centre, and at 2 mil of windage left and right on the 10-mil horizontal stadia.

Trademarks and design are the property of Horus Vision (www.HorusVision.com).
